# Hollowmere Real Assets Fund II: fund fact sheet

This fact sheet summarises the main terms of Hollowmere Real Assets Fund II, fund id HCP-04, a closed-end private-markets fund managed by Hollowmere Capital Partners. It is a summary for limited partners. The fund's governing documents apply if they differ.

## Strategy

HCP-04 invests in real assets: logistics buildings such as warehouses and distribution centres, and land held for development or long-term lease. The fund bought assets near freight routes and the harbour, improved them through lettings and refurbishment, and now sells them as they mature.

The portfolio is mostly stabilised buildings with tenants on multi-year leases, plus a smaller number of land parcels. The fund no longer buys new assets. Its work now is managing leases, preparing sales and returning proceeds to investors.

## Key facts

- Fund id: HCP-04
- Vintage year: 2016
- Fund size: 260m USD in total commitments
- Reporting currency: US dollars
- Status: harvesting
- Called to date: 99.2 percent of commitments

With 99.2 percent called, very little undrawn commitment remains. The latest call, Call 12, was 0.8 percent of commitments for fund expenses and the final management fee.

## Investment period and term

The investment period ended some years ago. The fund's term is 10 years from the final closing, and the general partner may extend it by up to two one-year periods. Extensions need the advisory committee's consent for the first year and a majority of limited partners for the second.

## Fees and carry

The management fee is 1.5 percent a year on net invested capital, meaning invested capital after deducting the cost of assets already sold. The fee therefore falls as assets are sold. It is charged quarterly in advance.

Carried interest is 20 percent of profits, paid only after limited partners have received back their called capital and a preferred return of 8 percent a year, the hurdle. Carry is subject to clawback at the end of the fund if the general partner has received more than its share.

## Distributions

As assets are sold, proceeds are distributed. Each distribution notice states the amount per unit, record date and payment date, and how the amount splits between return of capital and gains. The distribution waterfall guide explains the order in which proceeds are shared between investors and the general partner. The fund does not comment on tax treatment; investors should ask their own advisers.

## Reporting

Quarterly statements are posted to the portal 45 days after quarter end. Audited annual accounts follow 120 days after year end. The final statement and final accounts are issued after the fund is wound up.
