# Hollowmere Harbour Infrastructure II: fund fact sheet

This fact sheet summarises the main terms of Hollowmere Harbour Infrastructure II, fund id HCP-02, a closed-end private-markets fund managed by Hollowmere Capital Partners. It is a summary for limited partners. If it differs from the fund's governing documents, the governing documents apply.

## Strategy

HCP-02 invests in income-producing infrastructure: ports and quay facilities, cold and dry storage, rail sidings and road transport links. The fund buys stakes in operating assets with long contracts or regulated revenues, and aims to pay steady distributions from operations as well as gains on sale.

The portfolio is built around assets that serve the region's trade routes. The fund favours assets with inflation-linked contracts and limits exposure to any one counterparty to 20 percent of fund size. Development risk is kept small: no more than 10 percent of commitments may go to assets not yet in operation.

## Key facts

- Fund id: HCP-02
- Vintage year: 2018
- Fund size: 350m EUR in total commitments
- Reporting currency: euro
- Status: harvesting
- Called to date: 96.5 percent of commitments

HCP-02 reports its NAV, NAV per unit and distributions in euro. The other three Hollowmere funds report in US dollars.

## Investment period and term

The investment period has ended. The fund now makes follow-on investments in existing assets only, pays expenses and fees, and sells or refinances assets as they mature. Fewer capital calls are expected from here. The latest, Call 11, was for follow-on expenses and the quarterly management fee.

The fund's term is 12 years from the final closing. Extensions are possible only with the advisory committee's consent, as set out in the governing documents.

## Fees and carry

The management fee is 1.25 percent a year on invested capital. It is charged quarterly in advance and funded through capital calls.

Carried interest is 15 percent of profits, paid only after limited partners have received back their called capital and a preferred return of 6 percent a year, the hurdle. Because the fund pays distributions during its life, carry is calculated on the whole fund, not asset by asset, and is subject to clawback at the end of the fund.

## Distributions

As a harvesting fund, HCP-02 pays distributions when assets generate cash or are sold. Each distribution notice states the amount per unit, the record date, the payment date and how the amount is split between return of capital and gains. The split matters for an investor's own records, and the distribution waterfall guide explains how it is worked out. The fund does not comment on the tax treatment; investors should ask their own advisers.
