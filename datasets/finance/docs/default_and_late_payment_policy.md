# Default and late payment policy

This policy applies to every limited partner in the four Hollowmere funds (HCP-01 to HCP-04). It explains what happens when a capital call is not paid in full and on time. It does not replace the limited partnership agreement of each fund; if the two ever differ, the agreement applies.

## When a payment counts as on time

A capital call gives investors 14 days between the notice date and the due date. A wire counts as on time only if the full amount has arrived in the fund's collection account by 15:00 on the due date. A wire sent on the due date but received after 15:00 is treated as received on the next business day. Weekends and public holidays are not business days. Investors who are close to the cut-off are encouraged to wire a day early and to ask their bank for a confirmation message.

## Late payment interest

Any part of a capital call that is overdue accrues simple interest at 9 percent a year. Interest is counted daily from the due date up to the day the payment arrives, using a 365 day year. It is charged on the overdue amount only, it is not compounded, and it is paid to the fund, not to the manager. Interest is added to the amount the investor owes and is shown on the next quarterly statement. As an example, an overdue amount of 100,000 USD paid 10 days late would carry about 246 USD of interest.

## Reminders and the default notice

The investor relations desk sends a payment reminder on the first business day after the due date. If the call is still unpaid after 10 business days, the fund sends a formal default notice to the investor and to the contact addresses on file. The default notice states the overdue amount, the interest to date, and the deadline to cure. The cure period is a further 10 business days from the date of the default notice.

## Remedies if the default is not cured

If the overdue amount and interest are not paid within the cure period, the general partner may use any of the remedies set out in the fund's partnership agreement. These can include:

- Reducing or withholding future distributions until the overdue amount and interest are cleared.
- Charging the investor for reasonable costs of collection.
- Reducing the investor's share in the fund.
- Requiring the sale of the investor's interest, on terms the general partner considers fair.

The general partner decides which remedy applies. Remedies are applied to the defaulting investor only and are never applied without a default notice first.

## Requests for more time

The fund does not offer standard payment extensions. An investor facing a genuine difficulty should contact the investor relations desk before the due date. The desk will pass the request to the general partner, who may agree a short extension in writing. Nothing is agreed until it is confirmed in writing, and interest still runs unless the confirmation says otherwise.

## What the assistant and the desk cannot do

The assistant cannot waive interest, extend a due date or change a default status. Neither the assistant nor the desk can give advice on whether to pay, delay or fund a call another way. Investors should speak to their own advisers about those decisions. The desk is open Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 17:00, and is closed on public holidays.
