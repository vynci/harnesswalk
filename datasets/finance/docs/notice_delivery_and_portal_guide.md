# Notice delivery and investor portal guide

This guide explains how limited partners receive notices from Hollowmere Capital Partners and how to manage their delivery settings. It applies to all four funds, HCP-01 to HCP-04.

## How notices are delivered

Every notice is first posted to the investor portal. Capital call notices, distribution notices, quarterly statements, annual accounts and meeting invitations are all posted at 9:00 on the notice date. When a new document is posted, a portal alert appears at the top of your dashboard. The portal is the official record. A notice is treated as delivered once it is posted there, even if you have not opened it.

## Delivery options

There are two delivery options for each investor:

- **Portal only.** Notices are posted to the portal and you receive a portal alert. This is the default.
- **Portal plus courier copy.** In addition to the portal posting, a printed copy is sent by courier to the postal address on file. The courier copy is a convenience. It does not change the notice date or the due date, which always run from the portal posting.

Because the notice date is the portal date, we recommend checking the portal alerts regularly during the weeks when a call or distribution is expected.

## Changing your preferences

To change your delivery option, tell the investor relations desk, which is open Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 17:00. Preferences you state in conversation with the assistant are noted for future conversations, but the desk applies the formal change. A change to delivery settings takes effect within five business days. You will see a confirmation alert on the portal when it is applied.

## Statement format

Statements are posted as a PDF by default. If you prefer a spreadsheet version of the schedule of investments, ask the desk to switch your format. The statement figures are the same in either format.

## Contact windows

If you would like the desk to contact you only at certain times, for example mornings in your time zone, ask the desk to note a contact window. The desk will try to follow it for routine matters, but urgent matters such as a missed capital call may be raised outside the window.

## Bank details and security

Changes to bank details need a signed form uploaded to the investor portal. Chat cannot change them. After you upload the form, the desk confirms by a call-back using the contact details already on file before applying it. Wire instructions appear only in capital call notices on the portal. If you receive any message asking you to send money to a different account, do not act on it, and tell the desk right away.

## Signing in and access

Each investor has a named portal user, and additional users such as an administrator or adviser can be added with a signed authorisation. Access is reviewed once a year. If you cannot sign in, use the portal's reset option or contact the desk. The desk and the assistant never ask for your portal password.
