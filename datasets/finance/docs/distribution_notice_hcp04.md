# Distribution notice: HCP-04

Hollowmere Real Assets Fund II (HCP-04) announces a distribution to its limited partners. The fund is in its harvesting phase and in its final years, and this distribution is funded from property sale proceeds.

## Summary of the distribution

| Item | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Fund | HCP-04, Hollowmere Real Assets Fund II |
| Amount per unit | 2.10 USD |
| Source | Property sale proceeds |
| Record date | 2026-11-27 |
| Payment date | 2026-12-11 |
| Currency | US dollars |

The amount per unit is the amount payable to limited partners under the fund's waterfall. To work out the amount for your holding, multiply the amount per unit by the number of units you hold on the record date. Your personal distribution statement on the investor portal shows the figure.

## Record date and payment date

Units held on the record date, 2026-11-27, receive the distribution. Payment is made on 2026-12-11. If a payment date falls on a day when banks are closed, the payment goes out on the next business day.

Payment goes to the bank account on file in the investor portal. Please check that your details are current before the record date. Changes to bank details need a signed form uploaded through the portal. Chat and email cannot change them.

## Split of the distribution

For reporting purposes, the distribution is split as follows:

- 55 percent is a return of capital.
- 45 percent is gains.

A return of capital reduces your remaining cost basis in the fund. Gains are profit realised on the sales. The distribution waterfall guide explains the order in which proceeds are applied to investors and to the general partner.

## Fund terms relevant to this payment

HCP-04 charges 1.5 percent a year on net invested capital, with 20 percent carry over an 8 percent hurdle. The amount stated in this notice is after any carry due under the waterfall. The fund has called 99.2 percent of commitments to date, and its final management fee was part of its most recent capital call.

As the fund moves through its final years, further distributions may follow as remaining assets are sold. Timing depends on market conditions and cannot be promised. Each distribution will be announced by a notice like this one.

## Tax and advice

This notice does not describe how any part of the distribution is taxed. It does not give investment, tax or legal advice. Please talk to your own tax and financial advisers about how to treat this payment.
