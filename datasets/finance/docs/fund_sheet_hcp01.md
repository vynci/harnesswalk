# Hollowmere Growth Partners III: fund fact sheet

This fact sheet summarises the main terms of Hollowmere Growth Partners III, fund id HCP-01, a closed-end private-markets fund managed by Hollowmere Capital Partners. It is written for limited partners and is a summary only. If this sheet and the fund's governing documents differ, the governing documents apply.

## Strategy

HCP-01 makes growth equity investments in mid-sized, privately held companies that already earn revenue and need capital to expand. Typical uses are opening new sites, entering new regions, building out a product line or buying a smaller competitor. The fund takes significant minority or joint-control positions and works with management on hiring, reporting and planning.

The fund aims to hold a concentrated portfolio, usually between ten and fourteen companies. No single company may exceed 15 percent of total commitments at the time of investment without the advisory committee's approval. The fund does not invest in listed securities except for short-term cash management.

## Investment period and term

The investment period lasts five years from the final closing. During this period the fund may make new investments and call capital for them. After the investment period ends, the fund may call capital only for follow-on investments in existing portfolio companies, fund expenses and fees.

The fund's term is 10 years from the final closing. The general partner may extend the term by up to two one-year periods. The first extension needs the advisory committee's consent. The second extension needs the consent of limited partners holding a majority of commitments.

## Fees and carry

The management fee is 1.75 percent a year on commitments during the investment period. It is charged quarterly in advance and is funded through capital calls. After the investment period, the fee base steps down to invested capital, and the rate is stepped down in the governing documents.

Carried interest is 20 percent of profits, paid only after limited partners have received back their called capital and a preferred return of 8 percent a year, known as the hurdle. A catch-up applies after the hurdle, as set out in the distribution waterfall guide. Carry is subject to a clawback if the general partner has received more than its share when the fund is finally wound up.

## Capital calls

The fund calls capital by written notice. Each notice is posted to the investor portal and gives investors 14 days between the notice date and the due date. Unpaid amounts are covered by the default and late payment policy. As of the latest call (Call 9), 78 percent of commitments have been called, which leaves 22 percent available for follow-on investments, fees and expenses.

## Reporting

Limited partners receive quarterly statements on the portal 45 days after each quarter end, and audited annual accounts 120 days after year end. The fund's NAV and NAV per unit are stated as of each quarter end. For the latest figures, ask the assistant to look up the fund's NAV.
