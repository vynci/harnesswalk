# Distribution notice: HCP-02

Hollowmere Harbour Infrastructure II (HCP-02) announces a distribution to its limited partners. The fund is in its harvesting phase, and this distribution comes from the proceeds of an asset sale.

## Summary of the distribution

| Item | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Fund | HCP-02, Hollowmere Harbour Infrastructure II |
| Amount per unit | 3.20 EUR |
| Source | Asset sale proceeds |
| Record date | 2026-10-30 |
| Payment date | 2026-11-13 |
| Currency | Euro |

HCP-02 states its NAV and distributions in euro. Investors whose accounts are held in another currency should check the currency conversion arrangements on file with the investor relations desk.

## Record date and payment date

Investors who hold units on the record date, 2026-10-30, are entitled to this distribution. Units transferred after the record date carry no right to this distribution unless the transfer agreement states otherwise and the general partner has consented.

Payment is made on 2026-11-13 to the bank account on file in the investor portal. If your bank details need to be updated, do so well before the payment date through the portal, because changes need a signed form and are not made by chat.

## Split of the distribution

The distribution is divided into two parts for reporting purposes:

- 70 percent is a return of capital.
- 30 percent is gains.

Return of capital reduces your remaining cost basis in the fund. This is an accounting description of how the amount is applied against the capital you invested. The gains portion represents profit realised on the sale. The full explanation of how a distribution is split is in the distribution waterfall guide.

## What happens to carry

This distribution has been prepared in line with the fund's waterfall. HCP-02 charges 15 percent carry over a 6 percent hurdle. The amount per unit stated in this notice is the amount payable to limited partners after any carry due to the general partner under the waterfall has been taken into account.

## Tax and other advice

This notice does not cover how any part of the distribution is taxed in your own country or situation. It does not give investment, tax or legal advice. Please consult your own advisers on the treatment of this payment.

Your year-end tax statement for the fund will include the details your adviser needs, and it will be posted to the portal in line with the investor reporting calendar.
