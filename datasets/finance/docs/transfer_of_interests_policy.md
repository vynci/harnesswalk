# Transfer of interests policy

This policy sets out the rules for transferring a limited partner interest in a Hollowmere Capital Partners fund. It applies to HCP-01 to HCP-04. It is a summary for investors. The fund's partnership agreement is the governing document, and nothing here is legal or tax advice.

## General rule

A limited partner may not sell, assign, pledge or otherwise transfer an interest in a fund, in whole or in part, without the written consent of the general partner. The general partner may refuse consent, and is not required to give a reason, but will act reasonably and in good faith.

## Notice and timing

To request a transfer, the limited partner must give the general partner at least 30 days notice in writing through the investor portal. The notice must name the proposed transferee, the interest to be transferred and the proposed date. The 30 days run from the date the portal records the request as complete.

## Blackout around capital calls

No transfer will be processed in the period of 10 business days around a capital call due date, counted as five business days before and five after. This avoids confusion about who owes the call. If a transfer would otherwise complete in that window, it is moved to the first business day after the window ends.

## Transferee onboarding

The proposed transferee must pass the same onboarding checks as any new investor, including identity verification, anti-money-laundering review and confirmation that they are eligible to invest. The transferee must sign a joinder agreement accepting the terms of the partnership agreement. Transfers to affiliates of the existing investor still require the checks, though they are usually completed faster.

## Unfunded commitment

A transfer moves the whole of the transferor's rights and obligations for the interest transferred, including any unfunded commitment. The transferee becomes responsible for future capital calls on that interest. The transferor remains responsible for any call that was due before the transfer date.

## Administrative fee

An administrative fee of 2,500 USD applies to each approved transfer of interest. It is paid by the transferor, or as agreed between the parties, and covers onboarding checks, legal review and updating the register. The fee is not refunded if the transfer is later withdrawn after approval.

## Partial transfers and minimums

A partial transfer is allowed only if both the retained and the transferred interests are at least 500,000 USD of commitment each, or the equivalent in euro for HCP-02. Smaller partial transfers are not accepted.

## Late-stage funds

For funds that are harvesting and close to the end of their term, such as HCP-04, the general partner may decline transfers in the final 12 months of the fund's term, since the remaining life is short and the administrative work is the same.

## Questions

For help with a transfer request, contact the investor relations desk, open Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 17:00. The desk can explain the steps, but cannot give an opinion on whether a transfer is a good idea.
