# Investor reporting calendar

This calendar shows when Hollowmere Capital Partners sends statements, accounts and notices to limited partners. All documents are posted to the investor portal. The portal posts new documents at 9:00 on the date shown, and you receive a portal alert when something new is available.

## Quarterly statements

Quarterly statements show your commitment, capital called, distributions received, the fund's NAV and your share of it. They are posted 45 days after each quarter end. The schedule for 2026 is:

- Q1 (31 March): posted by 15 May 2026.
- Q2 (30 June): posted by 14 August 2026.
- Q3 (30 September): posted by 14 November 2026.
- Q4 (31 December): posted by 14 February 2027.

NAV figures in these statements are as of the quarter end date. If a valuation is revised after a statement is issued, the change appears in the next quarter's statement.

## Audited annual accounts

Each fund's audited annual accounts are posted 120 days after the year end of 31 December. For the year ending 2026, that means by 30 April 2027. The accounts include the auditor's report, the fund's balance sheet and a schedule of investments. Accounts are final once posted. Questions about them can be sent to the investor relations desk.

## Capital call notices

A capital call notice is posted at 9:00 on the notice date and gives investors 14 days before the due date. Each notice states the call number, percent of commitment, due date, purpose and the wire cut-off. Wires must arrive by 15:00 on the due date to count as on time. Late payments are covered in the default and late payment policy.

## Distribution notices

Distribution notices are posted at least 10 business days before the payment date. Each notice states the amount per unit, the record date, the payment date and the split between return of capital and gains. The record date is the day on which your holding determines your share of the distribution.

## Annual investor meeting

Each fund holds one annual investor meeting, normally in the second quarter. The invitation is posted to the portal at least 21 days before the meeting. Slides and a short written summary are posted within five business days afterwards.

## Changes to the calendar

If a date moves, for example because of a public holiday or a valuation delay, a notice is posted to the portal with the new date and the reason. Check the portal alerts or ask the investor relations desk, open Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 17:00.
