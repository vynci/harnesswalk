# Hollowmere Credit Partners I: fund fact sheet

This fact sheet summarises the main terms of Hollowmere Credit Partners I, fund id HCP-03, a closed-end private-markets fund managed by Hollowmere Capital Partners. It is a summary for limited partners, and the fund's governing documents apply if they differ.

## Strategy

HCP-03 is a private credit fund. It lends directly to mid-sized companies that are too small or too specialised for the public bond market. Most loans are senior secured, with a smaller share in second-ranking or mezzanine loans for higher returns. Typical loan terms run from three to six years, and most pay a floating rate.

The fund spreads risk across sectors and borrowers. No single borrower may exceed 8 percent of fund size at the time of the loan. The fund does not lend to companies in sectors the governing documents exclude, and loans are monitored quarterly with covenant tests and borrower reporting.

## Investment period and term

The fund is still in its investment period, during which it originates new loans and calls capital for them. Because loans are repaid over time, the fund may reinvest some repayments during the investment period, within the limits set out in the governing documents.

The fund's term is 7 years from the final closing. Because loans repay and pay interest throughout, the fund starts paying income earlier than an equity fund usually does.

## Fees and carry

The management fee is 1.0 percent a year on invested capital. It is charged quarterly in advance and funded through capital calls.

Carried interest is 12.5 percent of profits, paid only after limited partners have received back their called capital and a preferred return of 7 percent a year, the hurdle. Carry is subject to clawback if the general partner has been paid more than its entitlement at the end of the fund.

## Capital calls

HCP-03 calls capital as loans are originated, so calls tend to come in steps. Each notice gives 14 days between the notice date and the due date. The latest call, Call 4, was 8.5 percent of commitments for new loan originations and fund expenses. After it, 41.5 percent of commitments have been called to date.

Late payment is handled under the default and late payment policy, which sets interest on overdue amounts and the steps that follow.

## Reporting

Quarterly statements are posted to the investor portal 45 days after quarter end. Audited annual accounts follow 120 days after year end. Each statement shows NAV, NAV per unit, called capital, distributions to date and a loan portfolio summary.
