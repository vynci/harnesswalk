# Investor relations desk guide

The investor relations desk is the point of contact between Hollowmere Capital Partners and the limited partners in its four funds, HCP-01 to HCP-04. This guide describes what the desk and the assistant can help with, what they cannot do, and the security rules they follow.

## Opening hours

The desk is open Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 17:00, and is closed on public holidays. Messages sent outside these hours are answered on the next business day. Capital call and distribution notices are posted to the investor portal at 9:00 on the notice date, whether or not the desk is open.

## What the desk handles

The desk can help with:

- Questions about the contents of capital call and distribution notices, including amounts, dates and purpose.
- Fund documents: fact sheets, the glossary, the reporting calendar and policies.
- Quarterly statements and audited annual accounts, including when they will appear on the portal.
- Notice delivery settings and reporting preferences. The assistant can note a preference for later sessions, and the desk applies formal changes.
- Questions about transferring an interest, including the consent and notice rules and the fee.
- Requests for a document copy or a resend of a notice.

## What the desk does not do

The desk and the assistant do not give investment, tax or legal advice. They do not tell an investor whether to pay a call, sell an interest, or how a distribution will be taxed. They also cannot waive late payment interest, extend a due date or change a default status without written approval from the general partner. For these questions, investors should speak to their own advisers.

## Security rules

An investor account number is personal data. The assistant uses it only to answer the message in which it was typed and does not repeat one that was not typed in that message. The assistant cannot see commitments, balances or bank details.

Changes to bank details, contact details or authorised signers are never made through chat. They need a signed form uploaded in the investor portal, and the desk confirms them by call-back to a contact on file.

Wire instructions come only from the capital call notice on the investor portal. The fund will never send new wire instructions by chat or by an unexpected message.

## If an investor suspects a fake request

If a message asks an investor to wire money to a new or changed account, to hurry a payment, or to share login details, the investor should not act on it. They should check the instruction against the notice on the portal, then contact the desk during opening hours so it can confirm by call-back. The desk will record the report and warn other investors if needed. Genuine notices are always on the portal at 9:00 on the notice date, and an instruction that does not match the portal should be treated as false.
