# Capital call notice: HCP-03, Call 4

Hollowmere Credit Partners I (HCP-03) hereby gives formal notice to its limited partners of Call 4 under the fund's limited partnership agreement. This notice is posted to the investor portal at 9:00 on the notice date. Please keep it for your records.

## Summary of the call

| Item | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Fund | HCP-03, Hollowmere Credit Partners I |
| Call number | Call 4 |
| Notice date | 2026-11-02 |
| Due date | 2026-11-16 |
| Amount | 8.5 percent of each investor's commitment |
| Wire cut-off | 15:00 on the due date |

Each limited partner is asked to pay 8.5 percent of its total commitment. The amount in money for your commitment is shown on your personal call statement on the investor portal. It is your commitment multiplied by 8.5 percent, rounded to the nearest cent.

## Purpose of the call

The proceeds will be used for new loan originations and fund expenses. HCP-03 is a private credit fund, and new loans are funded as opportunities close. Fund expenses include administration, audit and the management fee in line with the fund's terms.

After this call, the cumulative amount called will be 41.5 percent of total commitments. The fund remains within its investment period, and the remaining 58.5 percent is available for future loans, expenses and fees.

## How to pay

Payment is made by wire in US dollars. Wire instructions are available only on the investor portal, in the call statement for this notice. Hollowmere never sends wire instructions by chat or by an unsolicited message. The investor relations desk can confirm instructions by call-back.

If you receive any message that asks you to send money to a different account, treat it as suspect and do not pay. Contact the investor relations desk, open Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 17:00, before taking any action.

## Cut-off and timing

The fund's account must receive your wire by 15:00 on the due date, 2026-11-16. Payments that arrive after the cut-off are treated as received on the next business day. Investors have 14 days between the notice date and the due date, so please plan for your bank's processing times, including any approval steps inside your own organisation.

## Late payment interest

Any amount not received by the cut-off on the due date accrues simple interest at 9 percent a year, counted daily from the due date until payment is received in full. Interest is not compounded and is payable in addition to the amount called.

The default and late payment policy sets out what can follow if an amount remains unpaid. If you foresee a delay, tell the investor relations desk before the due date.

## Important notice

This notice describes the call only. It does not give investment, tax or legal advice, and it does not describe the credit quality of the fund's loans. For questions about your own position, please consult your own advisers.
