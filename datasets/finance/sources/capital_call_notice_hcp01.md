# Capital call notice: HCP-01, Call 9

Hollowmere Growth Partners III (HCP-01) hereby gives formal notice to its limited partners of Call 9 under the fund's limited partnership agreement. Please read this notice in full and keep it for your records.

## Summary of the call

| Item | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Fund | HCP-01, Hollowmere Growth Partners III |
| Call number | Call 9 |
| Notice date | 2026-10-12 |
| Due date | 2026-10-26 |
| Amount | 6.0 percent of each investor's commitment |
| Wire cut-off | 15:00 on the due date |

Each limited partner is asked to pay 6.0 percent of its total commitment to the fund. The amount in money for your own commitment appears on your personal call statement on the investor portal. It is calculated by multiplying your commitment by 6.0 percent and rounding to the nearest cent.

## Purpose of the call

The proceeds of Call 9 will be used for two things. First, follow-on investments in two existing portfolio companies, where the fund has the right to add capital to support planned expansion. Second, the Q4 management fee, which is charged according to the terms in the fund's fact sheet and the partnership agreement.

After this call, the fund will have called 78.0 percent of total commitments to date. The remaining 22.0 percent stays available for future investments, fees and expenses during the investment period.

## Payment instructions

Payment must be made by wire transfer in the fund's reporting currency, US dollars. Wire instructions appear only on the investor portal and in the call statement attached to this notice there. They are never sent by chat, post or any other channel.

To protect investors, the investor relations desk confirms the instructions by call-back on request. If you receive any message that asks you to wire money to a new or different account, do not pay it. Contact the investor relations desk, open Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 17:00, and confirm the instructions before sending funds.

## Wire cut-off

Your payment must reach the fund's account by 15:00 on the due date, 2026-10-26, to be counted as received on time. A wire that is sent on the due date but arrives after 15:00 is treated as received on the next business day. Allow for your bank's own processing time, including cut-off times for same-day international wires.

## Late payment

If payment of Call 9 is not received by the cut-off on the due date, the overdue amount accrues simple interest at 9 percent a year. Interest is counted daily from the due date until the day the full amount, together with the interest, is received. Interest is not compounded.

Repeated or prolonged late payment can lead to further steps under the default provisions of the partnership agreement. These are described in the default and late payment policy, which you can read on the portal. If you expect a problem meeting the due date, contact the investor relations desk before the due date and not after it.

## Questions

This notice does not give investment, tax or legal advice. For questions about how the call affects your own position, please speak with your own advisers. For questions about the notice itself, the amount in money or the wire instructions, use the investor portal or contact the investor relations desk during its opening hours.
