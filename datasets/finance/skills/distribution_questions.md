# Distribution questions

1. Identify the fund (HCP-02 and HCP-04 have recent distribution notices).
2. Read the distribution notice and the distribution waterfall guide from the knowledge passages. These hold the amount per unit, record date, payment date and the split.
3. Quote the amount per unit and both dates exactly as written, and cite the notice as [1].
4. For the return of capital share, give the percent and explain in one sentence that return of capital lowers the investor's remaining cost basis in the fund. Do not comment on how any of it is taxed; that is for their own tax adviser.
5. If the investor asks about NAV after the distribution, call `fund_nav` with the fund id and give the latest quarter-end figure.
6. If the notice does not cover the question, say so and offer the investor relations desk.
