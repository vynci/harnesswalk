# Investor preferences

1. Recognise a durable preference: phrases like "from now on", "please note", "always send", "I want ... going forward". A one-off request is not a preference.
2. Call `remember` with kind `fact`, a short key of two or three words (for example `reporting currency`) and the preference as the value in plain words.
3. Confirm in one sentence that you have noted it, and say it applies to future sessions. Do not promise a change to an official record; the investor relations desk applies formal changes.
4. When an investor asks about something they asked you to note before, answer from the remembered facts that appear in your context. Do not call a tool for it.
5. Never remember account numbers, bank details or anything the investor did not ask to be noted.
6. Preferences about notices and statements follow the notice delivery guide. Cite it if you explain how a change takes effect.
