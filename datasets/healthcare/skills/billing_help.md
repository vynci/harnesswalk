# Billing help

1. Read the `billing_faq` document with `read_doc` and answer only from it.
2. State the fee or rule plainly, with the number, and cite the passage as [1].
3. For late cancellation fees, also mention the 24-hour rule from the `appointment_cancellation_policy` document.
4. If the patient asks about a specific balance or statement, say you cannot see account balances in chat and send them to the patient portal billing page or the front desk.
5. Do not ask for card numbers or bank details. The clinic never takes payment details in chat.
6. Mention payment plans when the patient says a bill is hard to pay.
