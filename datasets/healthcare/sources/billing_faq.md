# Billing FAQ

This FAQ explains how Brindlemoor Family Clinic charges for visits that are not covered by insurance, how late cancellation fees work, and how to pay.

## What does an uninsured visit cost?

Patients without insurance coverage pay a flat fee for each visit. A standard visit costs 85 dollars. An extended visit, for example a long review of several conditions, costs 130 dollars. The visit type is decided by your clinician and is shown on your statement.

## What is the late cancellation fee?

Cancelling or moving an appointment with less than 24 hours notice costs 25 dollars. A missed appointment is charged the same fee. The fee is added to your next statement. It can be reviewed once per calendar year if the late cancellation was caused by an emergency. See the appointment and cancellation policy for details.

## Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. Balances over 200 dollars can be spread over up to 6 monthly instalments. To set up a payment plan, use the billing page in the patient portal or ask the front desk. Payment plans carry no interest and no setup fee. A missed instalment pauses your online booking until the instalment is paid.

## When do I get a statement?

Statements are issued on the first business day of each month and appear in the patient portal. A paper statement is mailed on request. Each statement lists visits, fees, payments received and the balance due. Payment is due within 30 days of the statement date.

## How can I pay?

You can pay in the patient portal or at the front desk of any site. The clinic never asks for card numbers or bank details in chat, by email or by phone. If anyone asks you for these details outside the patient portal or front desk, do not share them and tell the front desk.

## What if a bill is hard to pay?

Tell the clinic early. Beyond payment plans, the front desk can arrange extra time to pay and can explain community assistance programmes. Asking for help does not affect your care. You will not be refused an appointment because of a balance under 200 dollars.

## I think my statement is wrong

Check your statement against your visits in the patient portal. If something looks wrong, such as a fee you do not recognise or a visit you did not attend, contact the front desk through the patient portal billing page. A billing review takes up to 10 business days.

## What can the chat assistant tell me about billing?

The chat assistant can explain fees and rules like the ones above. It cannot see account balances, take payments, set up payment plans or remove fees. Those requests go through the patient portal billing page or the front desk.
