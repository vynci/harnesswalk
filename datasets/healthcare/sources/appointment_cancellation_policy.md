# Appointment and Cancellation Policy

This policy explains how patients of Brindlemoor Family Clinic book, move and cancel appointments, and what happens when an appointment is cancelled late or missed. It applies to all three sites: north, riverside and eastgate.

## Booking an appointment

Appointments are booked through the patient portal or at the front desk of any site. The portal is open at all hours. The front desk is staffed Monday to Friday 8:00 to 18:00, and Saturday 9:00 to 13:00 at the north site only. Every confirmed booking receives an appointment ref in the format `AP-1042`. Keep the ref handy: it is the quickest way for the front desk to find your booking.

Some services need a clinician request before they can be booked, for example most lab panels. The portal tells you when a request is required. Counselling intake can only be booked through the patient portal.

## Moving an appointment

You can move an appointment in the patient portal or by asking the front desk. Moving an appointment counts as cancelling the original slot, so the same notice rules apply. If you move your appointment at least 24 hours before it starts, there is no charge. Moving it with less than 24 hours notice is treated as a late cancellation.

## Cancelling an appointment

Please cancel as early as you can so that someone else can use the slot. A cancellation made at least 24 hours before the appointment time is free. A cancellation made with less than 24 hours notice is a late cancellation and carries a fee of 25 dollars. The fee appears on your next monthly statement.

The chat assistant cannot cancel or rebook for you. Cancellations must be made in the patient portal or at the front desk, so that you confirm them yourself.

## Late cancellations and the 25 dollar fee

The 25 dollar late cancellation fee applies when notice is under 24 hours. The fee can be waived once per calendar year if the late cancellation was caused by an emergency, such as an urgent hospital visit. Ask the front desk to review the fee and be ready to say briefly what happened. Waivers are decided by the clinic manager and are not available through chat.

## Missed appointments

A missed appointment (a no-show) is an appointment you did not attend and did not cancel. A missed appointment is charged the same 25 dollars as a late cancellation. If a patient misses three appointments within 12 months, the clinic may require them to book through the front desk only for the next 3 months. After that period online booking is restored automatically. The rule exists so that appointment slots are not lost to repeated no-shows.

## Waitlisted appointments

A waitlisted appointment is not a confirmed booking. The clinic will call if a slot opens, and the patient may accept or decline. Waitlisted slots are not charged if they are not taken up. Once a slot is offered and accepted, normal cancellation rules apply to it.

## Rebooking

After a cancellation, you can rebook right away through the patient portal. If the patient portal shows no slots, ask the front desk to add you to the waitlist for the service and site you prefer.

## Questions

Questions about a specific fee, waiver or statement go to the front desk or the patient portal billing page. The chat assistant can explain the rules but cannot change a booking, remove a fee or give medical advice.
