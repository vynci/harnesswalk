# Patient FAQ

Answers to the questions patients ask most often at Brindlemoor Family Clinic.

## What are your opening hours?

All three sites (north, riverside and eastgate) are open Monday to Friday 8:00 to 18:00. The north site is also open on Saturday from 9:00 to 13:00. Riverside and eastgate are closed on Saturday. All sites are closed on Sundays and on public holidays.

## How do I book an appointment?

Book through the patient portal at any time, or at the front desk during opening hours. Each confirmed booking gets an appointment ref in the format `AP-1042`. The chat assistant can look up an appointment by its ref, but it cannot book, move or cancel for you.

## Can I just walk in?

Walk-ins are accepted at two sites for a limited window. The north site takes walk-ins from 8:00 to 10:00 on weekdays. The eastgate site takes walk-ins from 16:00 to 17:30 on weekdays. The riverside site does not take walk-ins. Walk-in patients are seen in the order they arrive, and booked patients are seen first, so waits can vary.

## What should I bring?

Bring photo identification, your list of current medicines and any referral or clinician request if your service needs one. If you have an appointment for a fasting blood test, follow the preparation note in your booking. For vaccinations, wear a short-sleeved top.

## What if I need to cancel?

Cancel in the patient portal or at the front desk at least 24 hours ahead. A late cancellation costs 25 dollars. See the appointment and cancellation policy for the full rules.

## How do I get my test results?

Lab results appear in the patient portal 3 to 5 business days after your test. Your clinician explains the results at your follow-up visit. Staff and the chat assistant cannot interpret results.

## Why will the chat assistant not give medical advice?

The chat assistant is a front-desk assistant, not a clinician. It cannot give medical advice, make a diagnosis, suggest a dose or interpret symptoms. For a health question, book an appointment with your clinician or use the nurse advice line through the patient portal. If you think you are having an emergency, contact emergency services immediately.

## Can the assistant tell me about someone else's appointment?

No. Appointments and records are private. The assistant will not share another person's information, even for a family member. A person may share their own appointment details with a relative themselves.

## Is my personal information safe in chat?

Personal details such as a health number, email address or phone number are replaced with placeholders before the assistant sees them. The assistant is also checked so that its replies do not reveal anyone else's details. Even so, please share only what is needed.

## Who do I ask for help with accessibility?

Ask the front desk or see the interpreter and accessibility services guide. Interpreters and accessibility support are free of charge.
