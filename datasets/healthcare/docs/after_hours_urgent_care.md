# After-Hours and Urgent Care Guidance

This guide explains where to turn when Brindlemoor Family Clinic is closed or when a situation cannot wait for a regular appointment. It tells you where to go and does not give medical advice. For anything about your own health, your own clinician is the right person to ask.

## When the Clinic Is Open

All three sites are open Monday to Friday from 8:00 to 18:00. The north site is also open on Saturday from 9:00 to 13:00. Walk-ins are available at north on weekdays from 8:00 to 10:00 and at eastgate on weekdays from 16:00 to 17:30. Outside these hours, the clinic is closed.

## Emergencies: Contact Emergency Services

If someone is in immediate danger, contact emergency services right away. Do not use chat, the patient portal or the front desk first. Examples that should always go to emergency services include:

- chest pain or pressure
- trouble breathing
- heavy bleeding that will not stop
- sudden weakness, confusion or loss of consciousness
- thoughts of harming yourself or someone else

This list is only a reminder of when to call, not a full guide. If you are unsure whether something is an emergency, treat it as one.

## Non-Urgent Questions Outside Hours

For questions that are worrying but not urgent, you can use the nurse advice line, which is reached through the patient portal. A nurse will respond within one business day. The nurse advice line is not an emergency service, so please do not use it for urgent symptoms.

## Waiting for the Next Appointment

Some situations can usually wait for a regular booking, such as a routine check-up, a repeat prescription review or an ongoing condition that has not changed. You can find a slot in the patient portal, which gives you an appointment ref in the form `AP-1042`. If you are unsure whether to wait, ask the nurse advice line or your own clinician.

## Prescriptions Out of Hours

Prescription renewals take 7 business days and are requested only through the patient portal. If you are about to run out of medication, tell the front desk as soon as the clinic opens so a clinician can look at the situation. The clinic cannot renew prescriptions in chat or by phone message.

## Lab Results and Referrals

Lab results appear in the patient portal 3 to 5 business days after your test. Nobody at the clinic can interpret results through chat. If a result needs action, your clinician will contact you through the portal.

## What the Chat Assistant Can and Cannot Do

The front-desk assistant can share opening hours, find an appointment by its ref, explain clinic policies and point you to the right service. It cannot give medical advice, diagnose, discuss doses, share another person's records, or handle emergencies. If you describe urgent symptoms in chat, the assistant will tell you to contact emergency services.

## Accessibility and Language Support

If you need an interpreter, a quiet room or another form of support, tell the front desk when you book. Services are described in the interpreter and accessibility guide and in the site directory.
