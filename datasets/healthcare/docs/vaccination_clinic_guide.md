# Vaccination Clinic Guide

Brindlemoor Family Clinic offers seasonal, travel and routine vaccinations. This guide covers what is available, where, how to prepare and what to expect afterwards. Book through the patient portal or the front desk. No referral is needed for any vaccination.

## Seasonal flu clinic

The seasonal flu clinic runs from 2026-10-05 to 2026-12-18. It is held at two sites: north and eastgate. The riverside site does not give flu vaccines, because its space is used for the lab. Appointments take about 15 minutes, plus the observation wait described below.

People who benefit most from the flu vaccine include adults over 65, pregnant patients, people with long-term conditions such as asthma or diabetes, and anyone who lives with a person at higher risk. Your own clinician can tell you whether the vaccine is right for you. The chat assistant cannot give that advice.

## Travel vaccines

Travel vaccines need a consultation first, so that a nurse can match the vaccines to your route and dates. Book the consultation at least 6 weeks before you travel. Some vaccines need more than one dose, spaced weeks apart, so leaving it later can mean you are not fully covered by departure. Bring your itinerary and your vaccination history, which is also available in the patient portal.

## Tetanus boosters

Tetanus boosters are available at north and eastgate. Adults are usually advised to have a booster every 10 years, and sooner after a deep or dirty wound. The nurse checks your vaccination history at the visit.

## Childhood schedule catch-up

If a child has missed a scheduled dose, the clinic can arrange catch-up vaccinations. A parent or legal guardian must attend. Bring the child's vaccination record if you have one.

## Before your appointment

For most vaccinations, there is little to prepare. Wear a short-sleeved or loose top so the nurse can reach your upper arm. Eat normally. If you feel unwell with a fever on the day, call the front desk to move the appointment; the 24-hour cancellation rule may apply, so give notice as early as you can.

## After the injection

Everyone waits 15 minutes in the observation area after a vaccination, so staff can help quickly if you feel faint or have a reaction. Common effects, such as a sore arm or mild tiredness, usually pass within a day or two. If you have severe symptoms, such as difficulty breathing, call emergency services immediately.

## Records

Your vaccination is added to your record and appears in the patient portal. You can request an official copy through the records request process, which is answered within 10 business days.
