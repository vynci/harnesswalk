# Lab Tests Guide

Brindlemoor Family Clinic runs a small laboratory collection service. This guide explains where tests happen, how to prepare, how to book and when you can see your results.

## Where and when

All lab tests are taken at the riverside site, in the Willowbank district. The lab is open from 07:30 to 12:00 on weekdays. It is closed on Saturdays and Sundays. If you cannot reach riverside, speak to the front desk, who can suggest the nearest option.

## Services

The lab handles fasting blood tests, urine tests, and cholesterol and glucose panels. Samples are sent to a partner laboratory for analysis, apart from basic urine dip checks done on site.

## Getting a test

Most panels need a clinician request. Your clinician can add the request during or after an appointment, and it appears in the patient portal. Once the request is on file, book a lab slot through the patient portal. Without a request, the lab cannot take the sample. If you think you need a test, book an appointment with your clinician through the portal.

## Fasting

Some blood tests need fasting. For fasting blood tests, fast for 10 hours before your appointment. Water is allowed and encouraged. Do not skip regular medication unless your clinician has told you to, and do not change doses yourself. If you are unsure, ask your clinician or send a question through the patient portal. Early morning slots are the easiest for fasting tests, which is why the lab opens at 07:30.

## On the day

Arrive 10 minutes early. Bring photo identification and, if you have one, your confirmation from the patient portal. Wear a top with sleeves that roll up easily. The sample collection itself takes about 5 minutes.

## Results

Results appear in the patient portal 3 to 5 business days after the test. You will receive a portal notification when they are ready. If a result needs follow-up, a clinician will contact you through the portal or by phone.

The chat assistant and front-desk staff cannot interpret results. For questions about what a result means, book an appointment with your clinician through the patient portal.

## Cancelling or moving a lab slot

Lab slots follow the same 24-hour rule as other appointments. Cancelling with less than 24 hours notice may carry a 25 dollar late cancellation fee. Change your slot in the patient portal as early as you can, because fasting slots are in high demand.

## Billing

Lab fees depend on the panel and on your insurance cover. Uninsured patients are quoted a price when the lab slot is booked. See the Billing FAQ for payment plan options.
