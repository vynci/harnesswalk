# Physiotherapy Programme

Brindlemoor Family Clinic offers physiotherapy at the north site, in the Harbour District. The service helps with back pain, joint problems, sports injuries and recovery after surgery. This guide explains how the programme works, how long it lasts and how to prepare.

## Do I need a referral?

No. You can book a physiotherapy assessment directly through the patient portal or at the front desk. Your own clinician may also suggest physiotherapy during a visit. The physiotherapist decides in the assessment whether the programme is right for you; the chat assistant cannot give that advice.

## The 8-week rehabilitation programme

The core programme runs for 8 weeks. It starts with an initial assessment, then continues with weekly follow-up sessions. At the assessment, the physiotherapist agrees goals with you and builds an exercise plan. Over the 8 weeks, the plan is adjusted as you progress. Some patients finish early; others are offered a short extension after review in week 8.

## Session lengths

- Initial assessment: 60 minutes.
- Follow-up sessions: 45 minutes.

Arrive 5 minutes early for the first session so you can finish the intake questions in the patient portal.

## What to wear and bring

Wear loose, comfortable clothing that lets you move freely, such as shorts or a loose top. Bring supportive shoes if the programme involves walking or balance work. Also bring any scans or letters you have about the problem, a list of your medication, and a water bottle.

## Waitlist rules

When the assessment calendar is full, patients are placed on a waitlist. A waitlisted booking shows the status "waitlisted" and is not a confirmed appointment. The clinic calls you if a slot opens. Patients who say they accept same-week slots are offered openings first. If you miss two waitlist calls in a row, the clinic removes you from the list and you can rejoin through the patient portal.

## Cancellations

Physiotherapy sessions follow the clinic's 24-hour rule. Cancelling or moving a session with less than 24 hours notice may carry a 25 dollar late cancellation fee. Three missed appointments within 12 months mean the next bookings must be made through the front desk for 3 months.

## Home exercises

The physiotherapist gives you a home exercise plan, which is also stored in the patient portal. Doing the exercises between sessions makes a large difference to results. If an exercise causes sharp pain, stop and tell your physiotherapist at the next session, or book earlier if you are worried. For urgent symptoms, contact emergency services.

## Accessibility

The north site has step-free access and a treatment room on the ground floor. If you need an interpreter or extra time, tell the front desk when you book so it can be arranged.
