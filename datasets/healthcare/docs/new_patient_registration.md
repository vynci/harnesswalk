# New Patient Registration

This guide explains how to register as a new patient at Brindlemoor Family Clinic. You must be registered before your first appointment at any of the three sites: north, riverside or eastgate.

## Who needs to register

Anyone who has not been seen at Brindlemoor Family Clinic before needs to register. Returning patients who have not visited for more than 3 years are asked to register again so that their details are current. Children are registered by a parent or legal guardian. A parent can register several children in one visit.

## Registering online

The fastest way to register is the patient portal. Create a portal account, then complete the registration form. Registration takes about 15 minutes. You will be asked for your name, date of birth, contact details, emergency contact, a list of current medications and any allergies. You will also be asked to upload a photo of your identification.

## Registering at the front desk

You can also register in person at any site during opening hours (Monday to Friday 8:00 to 18:00, and Saturday 9:00 to 13:00 at the north site only). Staff will help you complete the form on a tablet. Allow about 15 minutes. If you prefer paper forms, ask the front desk.

## What to bring

Bring photo identification, such as a passport or driving licence, and your Brindlemoor Health Number if you already have one. The Brindlemoor Health Number is the letters BH followed by two groups of four digits and a check letter, joined by hyphens. If you have never been issued one, the clinic assigns your number during registration. Bring a list of medicines you take and the name of your previous clinic, if you have one.

## Approval and first visit

Registration is reviewed by the registration team. Approval normally takes up to 2 business days. You will receive a notice in the patient portal once you are approved. You can book your first visit after approval. Booking is done through the patient portal or the front desk, not through chat.

## Privacy

The clinic stores your details in line with its privacy and records request policy. Your information is shared only with clinicians involved in your care unless you give written consent otherwise. In chat, personal details such as a health number, email or phone number are masked before the assistant sees them. The chat assistant cannot register you or change your details; use the patient portal or the front desk.

## Updating your details

Tell the clinic when your name, phone number, email or address changes. You can update these details in the patient portal at any time, or at the front desk with identification.

## Help with registration

If you need help with the form, ask the front desk for an interpreter or accessibility support. These services are free. See the interpreter and accessibility services guide for details.
