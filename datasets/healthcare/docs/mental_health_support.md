# Mental Health Support Services

Brindlemoor Family Clinic offers a small set of mental health services for adults at two sites: riverside (Willowbank) and eastgate (Eastgate Quarter). This guide explains what is available, how to book and what to expect. It is general information about how the clinic runs these services and is not clinical advice.

## What We Offer

The clinic runs three mental health services:

- **Counselling intake:** a first appointment of 50 minutes with a clinician, where you talk about what is bringing you in and what support would suit you.
- **Short-term counselling:** up to 6 sessions after your intake, each 50 minutes, with a counsellor at riverside or eastgate.
- **Stress and sleep workshops:** group sessions that run through the autumn. Dates are listed in the patient portal.

You do not need a referral for any of these services.

## How to Book

Counselling intake can only be booked through the patient portal. The front desk cannot book intake appointments by phone or in person, and the chat assistant cannot book them either. After you pick a slot, the portal gives you an appointment ref in the form `AP-1085`. Keep that ref, because it is how the front desk and the assistant find your booking.

A short intake form is also in the patient portal. Please complete it before your first visit so the full 50 minutes can be used for talking.

## Waiting Times

The typical wait for a counselling intake is about 2 weeks, although it can be shorter or longer depending on the site and the month. If you are waitlisted, the clinic will call you if an earlier slot opens. A waitlisted booking is not a confirmed appointment, so please do not plan around it until you receive confirmation.

## What to Expect at Your First Visit

Plan to arrive 10 minutes early. You will be shown to a private room. Quiet rooms are available on request, and you can ask for one when you book or at the front desk on the day. Interpreter services can be arranged in advance; see the interpreter and accessibility guide for details.

Your clinician will explain how your information is handled before you share anything. Conversations with your counsellor are kept within your clinical record under the clinic's privacy policy, and records are never shared through chat.

## Short-Term Counselling Sessions

After your intake, your clinician may suggest short-term counselling. Sessions are weekly or every second week, depending on availability. The 6-session limit exists so that more people can be seen. If you need longer support, your clinician will talk with you about other options and next steps.

## Workshops

Stress and sleep workshops are group sessions for adults. They are practical and educational in format. Spaces are limited, so book early through the patient portal. Workshops are not a substitute for an appointment with your own clinician.

## In a Crisis

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, or you are having thoughts of harming yourself, contact emergency services right away. Do not wait for a clinic appointment, and do not use chat. The front-desk assistant is not able to provide crisis support. For non-urgent questions, you can reach the nurse advice line through the patient portal.

## Cancelling or Moving an Appointment

Please give at least 24 hours notice if you need to cancel or move a counselling appointment. A late cancellation may carry a fee of 25 dollars. If cost is a concern, speak with the front desk about options before your visit.
