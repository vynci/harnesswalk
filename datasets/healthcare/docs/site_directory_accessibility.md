# Site Directory and Accessibility

Brindlemoor Family Clinic has three sites: north, riverside and eastgate. This directory lists where each site is, what hours it keeps, how to park and what accessibility features are available.

## Opening Hours

All three sites are open Monday to Friday from 8:00 to 18:00. The north site is also open on Saturday from 9:00 to 13:00. The riverside and eastgate sites are closed on Saturdays. No site opens on Sundays.

## North Site (Harbour District)

The north site is in the Harbour District and is the clinic's largest site. It offers primary care, vaccinations and physiotherapy. Walk-ins are taken on weekdays from 8:00 to 10:00.

Parking is free in the car park behind the building, which has 40 spaces. Two of these spaces are marked for patients with reduced mobility and are closest to the rear entrance.

## Riverside Site (Willowbank)

The riverside site is in Willowbank. It offers primary care, lab tests and mental health services. All fasting blood tests are done here, so expect early morning queues.

There is no on-site parking. Patients can park for two hours free on Willowbank Row, a short walk from the entrance. If your appointment may run longer, such as a counselling intake, please plan for a second ticket or ask the front desk for advice. Riverside does not take walk-ins.

## Eastgate Site (Eastgate Quarter)

The eastgate site is in Eastgate Quarter. It offers primary care, vaccinations and mental health services. Walk-ins are taken on weekdays from 16:00 to 17:30.

Parking is in the paid garage next door, which costs 3 dollars an hour. The garage has lifts that connect directly to the clinic's ground floor.

## Step-Free Access

All three sites have step-free entrances and at least one accessible toilet. Consulting rooms are on the ground floor at north and eastgate. Riverside has a lift to its upper floor, and any appointment that would normally be upstairs can be moved to a ground-floor room on request.

If you use a wheelchair or mobility aid, tell the front desk when you book. Staff can reserve an accessible room and clear the route in advance.

## Quiet Rooms

Each site has a quiet room that can be used before or after an appointment. It is a low-light, low-noise space for patients who find a busy waiting area difficult. Quiet rooms can be requested when you book through the patient portal or by asking at the front desk.

## Hearing Loops

Hearing loops are installed at the front desk and in the main waiting area of every site. If you use a hearing aid, switch it to the loop setting. Staff can also write instructions down or move to a quieter room if that helps.

## Assistance Animals

Trained assistance animals are welcome at all three sites, including consulting rooms. Please tell staff if your animal needs water or a rest area.

## Getting Help With Access

If something on this page does not meet your needs, tell the front desk or raise it through the patient portal. The clinic keeps a record of access requests and uses them when planning room allocation. See the interpreter and accessibility guide for language and communication support.
