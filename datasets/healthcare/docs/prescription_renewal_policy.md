# Prescription Renewal Policy

This policy explains how patients of Brindlemoor Family Clinic can renew a repeat prescription. It describes how the clinic's process works and is not clinical advice. Decisions about medication are always made by your own clinician.

## How to Request a Renewal

Renewals are requested only through the patient portal. Sign in, choose the prescription renewal option, select the medication and submit the request. You will see a confirmation in the portal that the request has been received.

The front desk can help you find the right place in the portal, but it cannot take a renewal request on your behalf by phone, in person or by a voice message.

## Renewals Are Not Handled in Chat

The chat assistant cannot renew, extend or approve a prescription, and it cannot pass a renewal request to a clinician. If you type a renewal request into chat, the assistant will tell you to use the patient portal. This keeps requests properly recorded and protects your privacy, since medication details should not be typed into chat.

## How Long Renewals Take

A renewal takes 7 business days from the day your request is received in the portal. Weekends and public holidays do not count. Because of this, please request a renewal when you still have at least two weeks of medication left.

Your clinician reviews each request. They may approve it, ask you to book an appointment first, or contact you through the portal with a question. Approval is not automatic.

## Medication Reviews

Every patient on repeat medication needs a medication review at least once every 12 months. If your review is overdue, your clinician may ask you to book one before the renewal is approved. You can book a review through the patient portal, and the booking will give you an appointment ref in the form `AP-1042`.

## Urgent Shortages

If you are about to run out of medication and cannot wait 7 business days, tell the front desk as soon as possible. Staff will pass the situation to a clinician, who will decide what can be done. Please do not wait until your last day of supply to raise this.

If you feel unwell because of a missed dose or a gap in your medication, contact your clinician through the patient portal or, if the situation is urgent, use emergency services. The clinic's front-desk assistant cannot give advice about doses or symptoms.

## Collecting a Prescription

Once approved, your prescription is sent to the pharmacy you chose in the portal. You will see a status update in the portal. The clinic does not dispense medication on site.

## Changing Your Details

If your pharmacy or contact details change, update them in the patient portal or at the front desk. Please do not type health numbers, emails or phone numbers into chat to make these changes, because chat cannot update your record.

## Fees

There is no fee for a routine renewal. A fee may apply if you miss a scheduled medication review without giving 24 hours notice. See the appointment and cancellation policy for the late cancellation fee of 25 dollars.
