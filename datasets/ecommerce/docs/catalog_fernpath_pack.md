# Catalog Sheet: Fernpath 45L Backpack

The Fernpath 45L is our everyday multi-day pack: big enough for a two or three night trip, light enough that you notice the difference after a few hours on the trail. This sheet covers its specs, the fit range, and how it compares to the 55L model.

## At a glance

- Price: 129.00 dollars
- Capacity: 45 litres
- Weight: 1.3 kg
- Rain cover: included, stored in the base pocket
- Torso range: adjustable from 43 to 53 cm
- Frame: lightweight aluminium stay with a ventilated back panel

## Features

The pack has a top lid pocket for small items, a zipped bottom compartment for a sleeping bag or wet gear, two stretch side pockets that hold a one litre bottle each, and hip belt pockets large enough for a phone or snacks. Daisy chains on the front let you lash on trekking poles or a wet jacket. A sleeve inside takes a hydration bladder, and the hose port exits on either shoulder.

## Fit and adjustment

The torso length is adjustable from 43 to 53 cm, which covers most adults from roughly 160 cm to 190 cm tall. To set it, measure from the bony bump at the base of your neck down to the line between your hip bones, then slide the shoulder harness to that number and fasten the hook-and-loop patch. The hip belt should sit on top of your hip bones, not your waist, so that about 80 percent of the load rides on your hips. The belt comes in two sizes. If you are between sizes, choose the smaller one and check that the pads wrap without gaps.

## Comparing the 55L option

The Fernpath 55L costs 149.00 dollars and weighs 1.5 kg. It adds 10 litres, which is useful for winter trips with a bulkier sleeping bag or for carrying extra food on a longer route. The frame and torso range are the same, so the fit advice above applies to both. If your trips are mostly one to three nights in mild weather, the 45L is usually the better balance of volume and weight.

## Care and storage

Empty every pocket after a trip and shake out grit. Hand wash with lukewarm water and mild soap, using a soft brush on the back panel. Do not machine wash or tumble dry. Let the pack dry completely, away from direct heat, before you store it with the straps loosened.

## Warranty

The Fernpath 45L carries a 2 year warranty against defects in materials and workmanship, such as a seam that fails, a buckle that snaps in normal use or a zip that separates. Normal wear, UV fading and damage from overloading are not covered. If you think your pack is defective, start a claim from your order. The return label fee is waived for defects.
