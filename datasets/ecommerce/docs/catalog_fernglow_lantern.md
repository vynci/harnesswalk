# Catalog Sheet: Fernglow Lantern

The Fernglow is Quillfern Outfitters' rechargeable camp lantern. It is built for tent ceilings, picnic tables and power cuts at home, and it is one of our most popular small items. This sheet lists the specs, the price and the return rules that apply to it.

## At a glance

- Price: 42.50 dollars.
- Output: 300 lumens on the highest setting.
- Runtime: 40 hours on the low setting.
- Charging: rechargeable, with a full charge taking about 3 hours.
- Weight: 280 grams, with a fold-flat hanging hook.
- Colours: sage green and slate grey.

## Light modes

The Fernglow has three brightness levels and a warm night mode. High gives the full 300 lumens and is best for cooking or packing a tent. Medium is a good all-evening setting. Low is the 40 hour setting and is soft enough to read by. Night mode uses a dim amber light that is easy on the eyes if you wake up in the dark.

## Battery and charging

The lantern uses a sealed rechargeable battery that is charged through its charging port. A full charge takes about 3 hours. Four indicator dots show the charge level, and they blink when the battery is almost empty. The battery is designed to hold most of its capacity through hundreds of charge cycles. If you store the lantern for a long period, top it up to about half charge every few months.

## Build and weather

The body is made of impact-resistant plastic with a frosted lens that spreads the light evenly. It handles light rain and splashes, but it is not designed to be submerged. Dry it off after a wet trip and let it air out before storing it.

## Returns and electronics

The Fernglow counts as an electronic item. If it is opened or used, it can be returned within 14 days of delivery. An unopened Fernglow follows the standard 30 day return window. The 6 dollar return label fee is waived if the lantern is defective or arrived damaged, for example with a cracked lens. If your lantern arrives damaged, contact support with photos and an order id so the claim can be reviewed.

## Warranty

The Fernglow is covered against manufacturing defects. For warranty questions, read the warranty policy or contact support, who can look up your order and walk you through the next steps.

## Care tips

Wipe the lens with a soft, dry cloth. Do not use solvents or abrasive cleaners, which can cloud the plastic. Keep the charging port clean and dry, and avoid leaving the lantern in a hot car for long periods, since heat shortens battery life.
