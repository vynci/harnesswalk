## What is in the set

The 25 cm skillet suits eggs, sausages and searing. The two pots are 1.5 and 3 litres, with loop handles that take a pot hook or stay clear of the flame. A single flat lid fits both pots, and the lid doubles as a shallow baking tray over coals. The 28 cm reversible griddle has a smooth side for pancakes and flatbreads and a ribbed side for vegetables and steak.

## Weight and handling

At 6.8 kg for the whole set, this is for car camping, home use and cabin trips, not backpacking. Single pieces are lighter: the skillet is about 2.1 kg and the 1.5 litre pot about 1.6 kg. Always use a thick glove or heat-proof pad, because the handles get as hot as the pan.

## Seasoning and cleaning

Every piece arrives pre-seasoned, but seasoning improves with use. After cooking, wipe out food with a paper towel, rinse with hot water and use a soft brush or scraper for stuck bits. A small amount of mild soap is fine. Never leave cast iron soaking and never put it in a dishwasher. Dry it fully on low heat, then rub a thin layer of cooking oil over the inside. Store it with a paper towel between the lid and pot so air can circulate.

If rust appears, scrub it off with steel wool, wash, dry and re-oil. The pan is not ruined. Rust is a care issue and a fixable one.

## Lifetime warranty

Emberhollow cast iron carries a lifetime warranty against defects in materials and workmanship, such as a crack from a casting flaw, a handle that separates from the body or a surface that flakes in normal use. The warranty does not cover rust from storage, chips from drops, cracks from thermal shock such as dunking a hot pan in cold water, or damage from using the pan as a hammer. For a claim, start from your order and describe the defect. The return label fee is waived for defects.
