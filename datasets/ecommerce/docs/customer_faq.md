# Customer FAQ

Quick answers to the questions we hear most at Quillfern Outfitters. If your question is not covered here, the support assistant can look up your order or search our policies for you.

## Support hours and contact

Our support team is open Monday to Saturday, 8:00 to 20:00 Eastern. We are closed on Sundays. Messages sent outside those hours are answered when we open, usually within the first hour of the next working day. You can reach support through the support form on our website or by chatting with the assistant at any time. The assistant can look up orders and tracking at all hours, but anything that needs a person, such as an approval to cancel or refund, is handled during support hours.

## How do I check the status of my order?

Give the assistant your order id, which looks like QF-20417 (the letters QF, a dash and five digits). It will tell you whether the order is processing, shipped, delivered or cancelled, what is in it and the total. You can also find the id in your confirmation message and in the Orders section of your account.

An order in processing has not left our warehouse yet. An order in shipped is with the carrier. An order in delivered has been scanned as handed over. An order in cancelled will not ship and has been refunded.

## Where do I find my tracking id?

Tracking ids look like QT-58120394 (the letters QT, a dash and eight digits). One is created as soon as your order ships, and it appears in your shipping message and on the order page. If your order is still processing, there is no tracking id yet. Once you have one, you can ask the assistant to track the shipment and it will report the carrier, the last scan and the expected delivery date.

## Can I change the delivery address?

You can change the address only while the order is still processing. Once it has shipped, the carrier already holds the parcel and we cannot redirect it. If the order has shipped to the wrong place, contact support right away. Sometimes the carrier can hold the parcel at a depot for pickup, but we cannot promise that. If the parcel is returned to us, we refund the order once it arrives back.

## Is shipping free?

Standard shipping is free on orders over 75 dollars. Below that amount, the shipping cost is shown at checkout before you pay. Standard shipping takes 3 to 6 business days, and express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days. Express is never free, but it is available on any order. The full details are in our shipping policy.

## Can I cancel an order?

You can cancel only while the order is in processing status. After it ships it cannot be cancelled, but you can return it once it arrives. Cancelling needs a human approval from our team, so the assistant will ask for it on your behalf and tell you when it has been approved. A cancelled order is refunded to the original payment method.

## How do refunds work?

A refund is available for a delivered order inside the 30-day return window. Like a cancellation, a refund needs a human approval before it is issued. Once approved, the refund arrives in 5 business days. Opened electronics have a shorter window of 14 days, and a 6 dollar return label fee applies unless the item is defective. See the returns and refunds policy for the full rules.

## What if my item arrives damaged or stops working?

Tell us as soon as you can, ideally with photos. A damaged or defective item can be returned with the label fee waived, and our warranty covers faults in materials and workmanship for 2 years on tents and packs, and for life on Emberhollow cast iron. If you are still inside 30 days, you can ask for a refund instead of a warranty repair.

## I saw a lower price after I bought. Can I get the difference?

If the price drops within 7 days of your purchase, we will adjust it. Ask support with your order id and we will review it. After 7 days we cannot adjust the price.

## Do gift cards expire?

No. Gift cards never expire. They cannot be exchanged for cash, so a gift card balance is not refundable as money. See the gift card policy for how to check a balance and what to do if a card is lost.

## What should I never share with support?

Never send full card numbers or passwords in chat. The assistant and our team will never ask for them. We only need your order id and, sometimes, a description of the problem.
