# Size and Fit Guide

Choosing the right size makes a big difference outdoors. A pack that is too long sits badly on the hips, a sleeping bag that is too short cramps your feet, and a tent that is too small turns a trip into a squeeze. This guide covers the three sizing questions customers ask most often.

## Measuring your torso for a pack

Pack fit depends on torso length, not overall height. To measure it, find the bony bump at the base of your neck (the seventh vertebra). Then find the top of your hip bones by putting your hands on your hips with thumbs pointing backwards. Have a friend measure along your spine from the neck bump to the line between your thumbs.

Quillfern Outfitters Fernpath packs fit torso lengths from 43 to 53 cm. Use the ranges below as a starting point.

- Small: torso 43 to 46 cm.
- Medium: torso 47 to 50 cm.
- Large: torso 51 to 53 cm.

If you are between sizes, choose the smaller one if you carry light loads and the larger one if you carry heavy loads, since a longer back panel spreads weight better.

## Adjusting the fit

Load the pack with about 8 kilograms before you adjust it. Tighten the hip belt first so the padding sits over the top of your hip bones and carries most of the weight. Then snug the shoulder straps so they wrap close without lifting off your shoulders. Finally, use the load lifter straps at an angle of roughly 45 degrees. If the pack feels like it is pulling backwards, tighten the lifters a little.

## Sleeping bag length

Mothdown sleeping bags come in two lengths.

- Regular: fits people up to about 183 cm tall, with a bag length of 190 cm.
- Long: fits people up to about 198 cm tall, with a bag length of 205 cm.

Extra length leaves empty space that your body has to warm, so avoid a long bag if a regular fits. Lie down inside the bag and check that your feet touch the end without pushing against it. You should be able to move your shoulders and hips freely.

## Choosing a tent size

Tent sizes are named for the number of people, but the labels are tight. For comfortable sleeping with a little room for bags, choose one size above the number of people. For example, two people with a lot of gear will like a 3-person tent.

- 2-person tent: good for one person with gear, or two light travellers.
- 3-person tent: good for two people with packs, or a small family.
- Allow about 60 cm of width per person for sleeping pads.

## A simple measuring tip

If you do not have a soft tape measure, use a piece of string. Lay it along the path from the neck bump to the hip line, then hold it against a ruler or a hard tape measure. Measure twice and use the larger number.

## Still unsure

Contact support during opening hours with your torso length, height and the kind of trips you take. The team can suggest a size, and items that do not fit can be returned within the 30 day return window if they are unused.
