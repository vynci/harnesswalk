# Catalog Sheet: Quillridge 3-Person Tent

The Quillridge 3-person tent is the middle of our Quillridge range, built for weekend trips and shoulder-season camping. It pitches in about ten minutes with one person and has two doors so nobody has to climb over a sleeping neighbour. This sheet lists the specs, what is in the bag, and how the warranty works.

## At a glance

- Price: 289.00 dollars
- Sleeps: 3 people, with a little room for packs
- Season rating: 3-season (spring, summer and autumn)
- Packed weight: 3.4 kg including poles, pegs and stuff sacks
- Doors: 2, each with its own vestibule
- Fly rating: 3000 mm waterproof
- Pitch: single-pole clip system, freestanding

## What is in the bag

Each tent ships with the inner tent, the fly, three aluminium pole sections, twelve pegs, four guy lines with tensioners, a repair sleeve for the poles and a footprint sheet. The two stuff sacks let you split the weight between two hikers: one carries the tent body, the other carries the fly and poles.

## Weather and comfort

The 3000 mm fly handles steady rain and the taped seams keep the floor dry in wet grass. The inner tent has mesh panels on both doors and a high vent at the back, which cuts down on condensation on cool mornings. It is not a winter tent. Heavy snow loads or strong gusts above 60 km/h are outside what a 3-season design is meant for.

## Sizing and room

The floor is 210 cm long and 190 cm wide at the widest point, so three regular sleeping mats fit side by side with a small gap. Peak height is 115 cm, enough to sit up and change without crawling. If you regularly camp with bulky packs or a dog, the Quillridge 4-person model gives you extra floor space, but it weighs more.

## Care

Pitch it on a flat spot and use the footprint sheet to protect the floor from stones. After a trip, shake it out and dry it fully before packing it, because a damp tent stored in its bag can mildew within a few days. Do not machine wash it. Wipe marks with a soft cloth and mild soap, and rinse with clean water.

## Warranty

The Quillridge 3-person tent carries a 2 year warranty against defects in materials and workmanship, such as a snapped pole from a manufacturing flaw, failed seam tape or a zip that splits in normal use. It does not cover damage from fire, a pitching accident or wear from ordinary use over many seasons. For a claim, start from your order and describe the defect. The return label fee is waived for defects, and the support team confirms the outcome after the claim is reviewed.
