# Catalog Sheet: Mothdown 0C Sleeping Bag

The Mothdown 0C is a three-season mummy bag with recycled fill, built for cool nights, shoulder-season trips and the edge of winter if you add a liner. This sheet lists the specs, packing tips and care, and explains how to choose the right temperature rating.

## At a glance

- Price: 96.00 dollars
- Comfort limit: 0C
- Weight: 950 g
- Fill: recycled synthetic insulation
- Length: regular, 190 cm (fits people up to about 183 cm)
- Shell: water-resistant ripstop with a soft lining

## What the 0C rating means

The comfort limit is the temperature at which a typical sleeper stays warm through the night in a tent, on a sleeping mat, wearing base layers. Your own comfort will change with the mat, your metabolism, food and wind. Cold sleepers should treat 0C as closer to 5C. Warm sleepers may be fine a few degrees below it. A good insulated mat matters as much as the bag itself, because the ground steals heat through the bottom of the bag.

## Features

The bag has a two-way zip with a draft tube to stop cold air leaking in, a contoured hood with a drawcord, and a small stash pocket inside for a phone or lip balm. A loop at the foot lets you hang it to air out. The recycled fill dries faster than down if it gets damp and keeps most of its warmth when slightly wet.

## Packing

The bag comes with a compression sack that packs it down to about 18 litres. For longer trips, store it in the bottom compartment of your pack and compress it just enough to fit. Do not leave it compressed for weeks at a time. Over many months this flattens the fill and reduces warmth.

## Care and storage

Air the bag out after every trip. Wash it two or three times a year at most, in a front-loading machine on a gentle, cool cycle with a mild soap made for outdoor gear, then tumble dry on low with a couple of clean tennis balls to break up clumps. Store it loose in the large cotton sack, never compressed in its stuff sack. A damp bag in a sealed sack will mildew.

## Choosing and returning

If you are unsure between the 0C and a warmer or colder bag, ask in chat and tell us the coldest night you expect. Unused bags can be returned within 30 days of delivery. The Mothdown carries the standard warranty against defects, such as failed zips or seam tears under normal use, and the return label fee is waived for defects.
