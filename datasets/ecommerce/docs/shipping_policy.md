# Shipping Policy

This policy covers delivery speeds, costs, carriers and tracking for orders from Quillfern Outfitters.

## Delivery speeds

Standard shipping takes 3 to 6 business days from the day your order leaves the warehouse. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days. Business days are Monday to Friday and exclude public holidays. Orders placed before the warehouse cutoff each business day are usually picked the same day, but large items such as Quillridge tents may need an extra day to prepare.

## Shipping costs

Standard shipping is free on orders over 75 dollars. Below that amount, a flat standard shipping rate is shown at checkout before you pay. Express shipping is always charged at the rate shown at checkout, and the free standard threshold does not apply to it. The threshold is based on the order total after any discounts and before tax.

## Carriers

We ship with two carriers. Marlowe Freight handles larger and heavier parcels, including tents and cookware sets. Brindle Parcel handles smaller parcels such as lanterns, packs and sleeping bags. The carrier is shown on your tracking page, and some orders use both if items ship separately.

## Tracking

Once your order ships, it gets a tracking id that looks like QT-58120394. You can ask the assistant to track it with either the tracking id or your order id, which looks like QF-20417. The assistant reports the carrier, the last scan, the status and the estimated delivery date. If your order is still processing, there is no tracking id yet, because it has not shipped.

Scans can lag by a few hours, especially overnight, so a parcel that shows no new scan in the morning is usually not lost.

## Delivery problems

If a parcel is marked delivered but you cannot find it, check with neighbours, the front desk and any parcel locker before contacting support. If it still has not turned up after one business day, contact support and the team will open a trace with the carrier. If an item arrives damaged, take photos and contact support. The return label fee is waived for damaged items.

## Delays

Severe weather and holiday periods can slow delivery. We do not guarantee delivery dates for standard shipping, but we do try to be accurate in the estimate shown. If your estimated date passes, ask the assistant to track the parcel for the latest scan.

## Changes after shipping

Once an order has shipped it cannot be cancelled or redirected. You can return it after it arrives, within the 30-day return window described in the Returns and Refunds Policy.
