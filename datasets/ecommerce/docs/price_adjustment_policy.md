# Price Adjustment Policy

If the price of something you bought from Quillfern Outfitters drops soon after your purchase, you can ask for the difference back. This page explains who can ask, how long you have and what is excluded.

## The 7 day window

Price adjustments are honoured within 7 days of purchase. The 7 days are counted from the date the order was placed. If the price of an item falls inside that window, you can request the difference between what you paid and the new price.

## How adjustments are paid

The difference is credited to the original payment method. If the order was paid partly or fully with a gift card, the credit goes back to that gift card. Credits usually appear within 5 business days after the request is approved.

## How to ask

Contact support during opening hours, Monday to Saturday from 8:00 to 20:00 Eastern. Have your order id ready, which looks like QF- followed by five digits. Tell the team which item changed price and, if you can, the new price you saw. The team will check the order and the current price before the adjustment is made.

## What is excluded

- Clearance items and final sale items, which are marked down for the last time.
- Bundles and sets sold at a bundle price, since their price depends on the items together.
- Items bought with a coupon or a promotional code that has already reduced the price.
- Gift cards, which are never discounted.
- Orders that were placed more than 7 days ago.

## Temporary promotions

If an item is on a short promotion, such as a weekend sale, a price adjustment is made only for the price difference during the 7 day window. If the sale price ends before you ask, there is no difference to pay.

## Returns and adjustments together

You can ask for either a price adjustment or a return, but the price adjustment does not extend the return window. If you decide to return the item after an adjustment, the refund is for the adjusted price, since that is what you paid in the end. Returns follow the standard rules, including the 30 day window for unused items and 14 days for opened electronics.

## Cancelled orders

Orders that are cancelled before shipping are refunded in full, so no price adjustment applies. If a price drops while an order is still processing, you may prefer to cancel and place a new order, but support can only cancel an order that is still in processing status.

## Questions

For any other price question, contact support with your order id. The team will confirm what is possible, and no adjustment is made until a person has reviewed the request.
