# Warranty claims

1. Ask what is wrong and which product it is. Look up the order with `lookup_order` to confirm it was delivered and when.
2. Search the knowledge base for the warranty policy: 2 years on tents and packs, lifetime on Emberhollow cast iron.
3. If the item is covered, explain the next steps from the policy and that the return label fee is waived for defects.
4. If the customer wants their money back instead and the order is inside 30 days, follow the refunds_and_returns skill.
5. Never promise a replacement date. Say the support team confirms it after the claim is reviewed.
