# Returns and Refunds Policy

This policy explains how returns and refunds work at Quillfern Outfitters. It applies to everything we sell online, including the Quillridge tent range, Fernpath packs, Emberhollow cookware, Mothdown sleeping bags and Fernglow lanterns. Support is open Monday to Saturday, 8:00 to 20:00 Eastern, and our assistant can look up your order and start a request at any time.

## The 30-day return window

You can return an item within 30 days of the delivery date. The clock starts on the day the carrier marks the parcel as delivered, not the day you placed the order. To qualify, the item must be unused, in its original packaging and include every part that came in the box. A tent that has been pitched once in the garden to check it, with no dirt or damage, is still treated as unused. A tent that has been taken on a trip is not.

If the 30 days have passed, we cannot issue a refund, but you may still have a warranty claim if the item is defective. See the Warranty Policy for details.

## Opened electronics

Electronics, such as the rechargeable Fernglow lanterns and headlamps, have a shorter window once the sealed packaging has been opened. An opened electronic item can be returned within 14 days of delivery. An unopened electronic item still follows the standard 30-day window. After 14 days an opened electronic item can only be handled as a warranty claim.

## Return label fee

Sending an item back with a prepaid label costs 6 dollars, which is taken from your refund. The fee is waived when the item arrived defective or damaged, or when we sent the wrong item. If you would rather arrange your own shipping, you may do so, but we cannot reimburse the cost and we recommend using a tracked service.

## How refunds are issued

Refunds are always sent to the original payment method. We do not refund to a different card, and gift cards cannot be exchanged for cash. Once a refund is issued, it arrives in 5 business days, although your bank may take a little longer to show it.

A refund is a destructive action in our systems, because it moves money and cannot be undone. For that reason every refund needs an approval from a member of the support team before it is processed. If you ask the assistant for a refund, it will check that your order is delivered and inside the window, then request the approval. It will only tell you the refund has been issued once the system confirms it. If the approval is declined, nothing is changed and your order stays as it was.

## Partial and multi-item orders

If you return only part of an order, the refund covers the price of the returned items. Free standard shipping that you qualified for with the original order total is not recalculated, so a partial return never costs you the shipping discount. Cookware sets are treated as a single item and are returned as a whole, because the pieces are priced as a set.

## Items that cannot be returned

We cannot accept returns on gift cards, on items that show signs of use, or on items that have been repaired or altered. Sleeping bags that have been washed or smell of smoke or pets are also excluded for hygiene reasons.

## Starting a return

Have your order id ready, which looks like QF-20417. Tell the assistant which item you want to return and why. If the order qualifies, the assistant will explain the label fee, ask for approval if a refund is needed, and tell you what to do next. If anything is unclear, the support team can review your case during opening hours.
