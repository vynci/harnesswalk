# Warranty Policy

Quillfern Outfitters builds and sells gear meant to be used outdoors, and we stand behind it. This policy describes what our warranty covers, what it does not, and how to make a claim.

## Warranty periods

Tents and packs carry a 2 year warranty, counted from the delivery date. This applies to the whole Quillridge tent range and to Fernpath backpacks. Emberhollow cast iron cookware carries a lifetime warranty for the original purchaser. Other products, including Mothdown sleeping bags and Fernglow lanterns, are covered by the standard 30-day return window and by the defect handling described below.

## What is covered

The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship. Examples include tent poles that snap under normal use, zippers that fail on a new pack, seams that split without being overloaded, and cast iron that cracks or has a casting flaw. If a covered item fails, we will repair it, replace it or, if neither is possible, refund it.

## What is not covered

The warranty does not cover normal wear and tear, such as faded fabric from long sun exposure or worn pack straps after years of carrying. It does not cover damage caused by misuse, for example pitching a tent in extreme winds beyond its rating, overloading a pack beyond its stated capacity, or leaving cast iron to rust in a wet sink. Accidents, fire damage and modifications are also excluded. Rust on cast iron that can be removed and re-seasoned is a care matter, not a defect.

## Starting a claim

To start a claim, have your order id ready (it looks like QF-20371). Tell the assistant which product is affected and what went wrong. The assistant will look up the order to confirm it was delivered and when, then check the warranty period. You may be asked for photos of the problem.

The assistant cannot promise a replacement date. Once the claim is reviewed, the support team confirms whether the item will be repaired, replaced or refunded, and gives you the timing.

## Return label fee

Normally a return label costs 6 dollars. For a warranty claim or any defective item, the return label fee is waived, so you pay nothing to send the item back.

## Warranty and the return window

If your order is inside the 30-day return window and you simply want your money back, you can use the returns process instead. See the Returns and Refunds Policy. Any refund still needs an approval from the support team. If the window has passed, the warranty is the right route.

## Proof of purchase

Your order record is your proof of purchase, so you do not need to keep a paper receipt. Gift recipients can make a claim using the order id from the gift giver, who can share it with them.
