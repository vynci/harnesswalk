# Services Desk Privacy Notice

This notice explains what the Aldervale Services Desk collects when you use the desk, how long
it keeps that information and what your rights are. It applies to the chat assistant, to the
application status service and to the office information the desk provides.

## What the desk collects

The desk collects only what it needs to answer your question. For an application status
check, that is the reference number, which begins with AV- followed by a letter and four
digits. For a programme question, the desk keeps the question you typed and the answer it gave.
The desk does not need a social insurance number, a full date of birth or a home address to
answer any question in chat.

## What you should not type into chat

Please do not type a social insurance number, a full date of birth or a street address into the
chat. The desk never needs these in chat, and it will never ask for them. If you do type one,
the desk replaces it with a placeholder before the assistant sees it or anything is stored, so
the number is not kept in the transcript.

## How long we keep transcripts

Chat transcripts are kept for 90 days. After 90 days they are deleted. Transcripts are used to
check that answers match the published programme rules and to improve the desk's service. They
are not used to make decisions about an application.

## Automatic replacement of personal details

Social insurance numbers, dates of birth and street addresses are replaced by placeholders
before anything is stored. The placeholders stand in for the original values, and the original
values are not kept in the transcript. This replacement happens automatically on every message.

## Access and correction

You may ask to see the personal information the desk holds about you, or to correct it. Send
your request to the desk's privacy officer through the contact form on the Aldervale online
portal. The privacy officer will confirm your identity before sharing or changing any record.
Changes of address are made on the online portal or at an office, with proof of residence.

## What we do not do

The desk does not sell personal information. It does not share personal information outside the
services of the Province of Aldervale, except where the law requires it. Application details are
used only to assess and pay the programme you applied for.

## Questions

For any question about this notice, use the privacy officer contact form on the online portal.
The desk's offices can also explain how to submit a request in person.
