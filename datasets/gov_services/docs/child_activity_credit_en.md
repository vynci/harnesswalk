# Child Activity Credit

The Child Activity Credit (CAC) helps families pay for registered sport, arts and learning activities for children. It is a credit paid on the fees a family has already paid. This sheet sets out the rules in full. The French version of this sheet, Crédit pour les activités des enfants, states the same rules and the same numbers.

## Who is eligible

A child qualifies if the child is under 16 on 31 December 2026. A child with a disability qualifies up to the age of 18 on the same date. The parent or guardian who paid the fees makes the claim and must live in Aldervale.

Only one claim is made per child per year. If two adults paid fees for the same child, the claim is made once, by the adult named on the receipts.

## Qualifying activities

An activity qualifies in one of two ways:

- A registered sport, arts or learning activity that lasts at least 8 weeks.
- A day camp of at least 5 consecutive days.

The organisation must be registered in Aldervale, or its receipt must show a registration number. Private lessons paid to an individual do not qualify unless the individual is a registered instructor.

## The amount

The credit is 50 percent of the fees paid, up to a maximum:

- 300 dollars per child per year.
- 500 dollars per child per year for a child with a disability.

## Documents

Send receipts that show:

- the name of the organisation,
- the child's name,
- the fees paid,
- the dates of the activity.

You may send up to 3 receipts with one claim. If the fees for a year are more than three receipts, send the three largest, which is enough to reach the maximum for most families.

## Deadline

Claims for activities held in 2026 are accepted until 31 December 2026. A claim sent after that date cannot be accepted for 2026.

## Worked example

Maya is 11 and takes a registered swimming programme that runs for 12 weeks. The family paid 480 dollars in fees. The credit is 50 percent of 480 dollars, which is 240 dollars. Because 240 dollars is under the 300 dollar maximum, the family receives 240 dollars.

Leo is 14 and has a disability. He attends a 6 day arts camp that cost 900 dollars. Fifty percent of 900 dollars is 450 dollars. The maximum for a child with a disability is 500 dollars, so the credit is 450 dollars.

## Questions and answers

**My child is 16 in March 2026. Do they qualify?**
Yes, if they were under 16 on 31 December 2026. The date that counts is the end of the year, not the date of the activity.

**Does a single-day activity qualify?**
No. A camp must last at least 5 consecutive days, and a programme must last at least 8 weeks.

**Can I claim for an activity that is still running?**
Yes, as long as the receipts show the fees paid and the activity runs during 2026.

For a status on a claim, the desk can check an application by its reference number. For any question about your own case, contact the desk through the online portal.
