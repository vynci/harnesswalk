# Home Heating Rebate

The Home Heating Rebate (HHR) helps Aldervale households pay for home heating. This sheet
sets out who is eligible, how much is paid, which documents to send, when to apply and how to
appeal. The French version of this sheet states the same rules.

## Who is eligible

To qualify, the household must meet all of these conditions:

- The applicant has been an Aldervale resident for at least 12 months before applying.
- The home is the household's principal residence.
- The household's income for 2025 was under 58,000 dollars.
- The household pays for its heating. If heating is included in the rent, a letter from the
  landlord replaces the heating bill.

Only one rebate is paid per household per heating season.

## How much is paid

The rebate is 450 dollars per household. It is 600 dollars when a member of the household is
65 or older.

## Documents to send

Send these three documents with the application:

1. One proof of residence. The accepted types are listed in the Proof of Residence sheet.
2. The most recent heating bill, dated within the last 3 months, or the landlord's letter if
   heating is included in the rent.
3. The 2025 provincial notice of assessment.

## When to apply

Applications for the 2026-2027 season are open from 15 September 2026 to 15 December 2026.
Late applications are not accepted.

## What happens after you apply

- A decision is made within 25 business days of a complete application.
- Approved rebates are paid by direct deposit within 10 business days of approval.

## Appeals

A refused application can be appealed within 30 days of the decision letter. The appeal is
made through the online portal or at any service office.

## Questions

The desk answers questions about this programme and checks application status by reference
number. Personal details such as a social insurance number are never needed in chat.
