# Senior Transit Pass

The Senior Transit Pass (STP) gives Aldervale residents aged 65 or older free travel on provincial buses and the harbour ferry, and half price on regional rail. It is a pass card, valid for two years, and it is available in English and French.

## Who can apply

You can apply if you are a resident of Aldervale and you are 65 years of age or older. Your age is checked from the date of birth on your photo ID, so the card is issued to you in your own name and matches the ID you show.

## What the pass gives you

- Free, unlimited travel on provincial buses and on the harbour ferry.
- 50 percent off regional rail fares.
- A pass that is valid for 2 years from the date it is issued.

## Documents you need

Bring or upload two things:

1. Photo ID that shows your date of birth.
2. One proof of residence that shows your name and the same address as your application. See the Proof of Residence sheet for the list of accepted documents.

## Your photo

The pass has a photo of you. The photo is taken at the Downtown Service Centre (ALV-CTR) or the Harbour Service Office (ALV-PORT), or by the Mobile Service Unit (ALV-MOB) on its monthly route. Office opening hours and closures are published by each office.

## Processing and delivery

Processing takes 15 business days from a complete application. The card is then mailed to the address on your file.

## Renewing your pass

Your pass is valid for 2 years. You can renew it up to 60 days before it expires. Renew early so that you do not lose travel time between cards.

## Lost or damaged cards

A replacement card costs 10 dollars. Report a lost card through the online portal or at any office, and the replacement is processed under the same 15 business day timeline.

## Questions

For help with an application, check its status by reference number, or ask a question about the programme, contact the desk through the online portal or visit any service office.
