# Proof of Residence

Most provincial programmes ask for one proof of residence. It shows that you live at the address on your application. This page lists the documents we accept, the documents we do not accept, and how to send yours. The French version, Preuve de résidence, states the same rules.

## What we accept

One of the following is accepted as proof of residence:

- an Aldervale driver's licence showing your current address;
- a lease, or a property tax notice;
- a utility bill for electricity, water or heating, dated within the last 3 months;
- a bank statement dated within the last 3 months;
- a letter from a shelter or a care home confirming your residence there.

## What we do not accept

These are not accepted as proof of residence:

- mobile phone bills;
- documents showing only a post office box address;
- handwritten notes.

## The 3 month rule

Bills and statements must be dated within the last 3 months before the date you send them. A bill from four months ago is too old, even if it shows your correct address. A lease and a property tax notice do not have this limit, but they must be for the current year.

## Name and address must match

The document must show your name and the same address as your application. If the name is slightly different, for example a middle name is missing, the desk may ask for a second document. If the address is different, make the change on the online portal first, then send the document.

## How to send it

You can send your proof of residence in one of two ways:

- upload it on the online portal, with your application reference;
- bring it to any service office during opening hours, or leave it at the drop-off point of the mobile unit.

Keep a copy for your records. The desk will tell you by letter if a document is missing, with a date by which to send it.

## Worked example

Amir applies for the Home Heating Rebate. His heating bill is dated four months ago. It is too old, so he finds a bank statement from last month showing the same address on his application. He uploads the statement on the portal with his reference number. Because the statement is dated within the last 3 months and shows his name and address, it is accepted.

Ana sends a mobile phone bill that shows her current address. It is not accepted, because mobile phone bills are not valid proof of residence. She sends her Aldervale driver's licence instead, which shows the same address, and her application can continue.

## Questions and answers

**Can I use my heating bill as proof of residence?**
Yes, if it is dated within the last 3 months and shows your name and address.

**My lease is from two years ago. Is it still accepted?**
Yes, a lease is accepted with no date limit, as long as it is still in force and shows your current address.

**Can I send a photo of the document?**
A clear photo or scan is accepted on the online portal. The document must be fully readable.

For a status on your application, the desk can check it by reference number. For any question about your own case, contact the desk through the online portal.
