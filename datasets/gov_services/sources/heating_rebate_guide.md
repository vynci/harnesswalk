# Home Heating Rebate: Complete Guide

## Overview

The Home Heating Rebate (HHR) helps Aldervale households pay for home heating during the cold
months. It is paid once per household per heating season. The rebate is 450 dollars for most
households, and 600 dollars when a member of the household is 65 or older. This guide sets out
who is eligible, which documents to send, how to apply, what happens after you apply and how to
appeal a refusal. The rules below are the same in English and in French.

## Who is eligible

To receive the rebate you must meet all of the following conditions.

- You have been an Aldervale resident for at least 12 months before you apply.
- The home is your principal residence, the place where you live most of the year.
- Your household income for 2025 was under 58,000 dollars.
- Your household pays for heating. If heating is included in your rent, your landlord can send
  a letter in place of a heating bill.

Only one rebate is paid per household per heating season, even if more than one person in the
home applies.

## Documents you need

Send these documents with every application.

- One proof of residence showing your name and the address on your application. Accepted proofs
  include an Aldervale driver's licence, a lease, a property tax notice, a utility bill dated
  within the last 3 months, a bank statement dated within the last 3 months, or a letter from a
  shelter or care home. Mobile phone bills and post office box addresses are not accepted.
- Your most recent heating bill, dated within the last 3 months, or the landlord letter if
  heating is included in your rent.
- Your 2025 provincial notice of assessment.

## Three ways to apply

You can apply in one of three ways.

1. Online: use the Aldervale online portal. You can upload your documents at the same time as
   the form, and you will see the status of your application in your account.
2. In person: take your documents to the Downtown Service Centre (ALV-CTR), the North Service
   Office (ALV-NORD) or the Harbour Service Office (ALV-PORT). Staff will check the documents
   with you and give you a receipt.
3. By mail: send your completed form and copies of your documents to the desk. Keep a copy of
   everything you send.

Applications for the 2026-2027 heating season are accepted from 15 September 2026 to 15
December 2026. Late applications are not accepted.

## What happens after you apply

Most decisions are made within 25 business days of a complete application. If documents are
missing, the desk writes to you and names each one, with a date to send it by. An application
that is still missing documents after that date will not be decided.

If your application is approved, the rebate is paid by direct deposit to the account on your
file within 10 business days of approval. You can check the status of your application at any
time with its reference number, which begins with AV-.

## Appeals

If your application is refused, the decision letter explains the reason. You can appeal within
30 days of the date on the decision letter. Include a copy of the letter and any new documents
that answer the reason for refusal.

## The five most common mistakes

1. Sending an old heating bill. The bill must be dated within the last 3 months. Check the date
   before you send it, or send the landlord letter if heating is included in your rent.
2. Using a mobile phone bill as proof of residence. Mobile phone bills are not accepted. Use a
   driver's licence, a lease, a utility bill or a bank statement instead.
3. Leaving out the 2025 notice of assessment. The notice is required with every application, even
   if you think the desk already has your tax information.
4. Applying twice for one household. Only one rebate is paid per household per heating season.
   If two people in the same home apply, the second application will be refused.
5. Applying after 15 December. Late applications are not accepted for the 2026-2027 season, so
   send your application early.

## Need help?

For questions about your own application, use the status check with your reference number. For
anything else, ask at any office or use the privacy officer contact form on the online portal.
