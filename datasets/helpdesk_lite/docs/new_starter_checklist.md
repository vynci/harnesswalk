# New Starter IT Checklist

This checklist explains how Pebblebrook Studio gets a new starter set up on IT, from the
manager's first request to the first morning at the desk. It applies to all three offices:
north, harbour and east. The IT desk is open 08:00 to 18:00, Monday to Friday, and the
harbour office desk runs 09:00 to 17:00, Monday to Thursday.

## 1. Before the start date: the manager opens a ticket

The starter's manager opens a ticket through the support form as soon as the start date is
agreed. The ticket should name the starter, their office, their start date and the software
they will need. The desk answers every new ticket within 4 working hours, so a ticket opened
a week ahead leaves plenty of time for the setup.

The ticket is the only way the desk knows a starter is coming. A message in a chat or a call
to the desk does not count, so please always use the form.

## 2. The day before the start date: accounts are created

The desk creates the starter's accounts the day before their start date. That covers the
staff network login, email, the file share and the video call kit booking for their office.
The starter receives a message from the desk with their login name and a link to the account
portal. They choose their own password on the portal; the desk never asks for a password
and never sets one for them over chat, email or phone.

If the starter cannot sign in on their first morning, they should first try the account portal.
A locked account unlocks by itself after 15 minutes. If it still fails, the starter opens a
ticket on the support form and the desk answers within 4 working hours.

## 3. Two working days after the ticket: the laptop is ready

A new starter's laptop is ready two working days after the manager opens the ticket. For
example, a ticket opened on a Monday gives a laptop ready on Wednesday. The laptop comes
set up with the company image, the VPN app and the printers for the starter's office. The
starter collects it at the desk during opening hours. At the harbour office, collection is
between 09:00 and 17:00, Monday to Thursday, so please plan the pickup around those times.

If the laptop is delayed, the ticket shows the new date under next steps. Please do not
chase by chat; the ticket is updated as soon as the desk knows more.

## 4. On the first day: a short setup list

Please work through this list on the first morning. Each step takes a few minutes.

1. Sign in to the laptop with the account you chose on the portal.
2. Connect to the staff network for your office. Use the guest network only for visitors. The
   guest network code is on the reception card and changes every Monday.
3. Add your office printer. The north office has NO-PRN-1 and NO-PRN-2, the harbour office has
   HB-PRN-1 and HB-PRN-2, and the east office has EA-PRN-1.
4. Open the file share and check that your team's folders show up. Files are backed up every
   night from 01:00 to 02:00, and a deleted file can be restored for 30 days.
5. Check that the VPN app connects from home if you will be working remotely. The VPN app
   reconnects by itself after a short drop.
6. Read the security basics page. Lock your screen whenever you leave your desk.

## 5. If something goes wrong

Check the service status first. The desk publishes the status of email, the VPN, printers,
wifi, the file share and video calls. A problem that affects everyone is usually already known
and may have a workaround listed. If the service is fine and your problem remains, open a
ticket on the support form and include what you were doing and what you saw on screen.

Out-of-hours urgent problems go to the on-call technician through the support form. Please
only use this for outages that stop your work completely.

## 6. Who to ask

Questions about accounts, laptops and access go to the IT desk through the support form.
Questions about your manager's ticket go to your manager first, who can see its status.
Please keep questions in the form so that the desk can answer them the same way for everyone.
