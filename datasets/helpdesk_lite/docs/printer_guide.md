# Printer Guide

This guide lists the printers in each Pebblebrook office, explains how to clear a stuck job, and covers what to do when a printer is offline or out of toner.

## The printers in each office

**North office**: two printers, named NO-PRN-1 and NO-PRN-2. Both are on the staff network and appear in your print list under those names.

**Harbour office**: two printers, named HB-PRN-1 and HB-PRN-2. HB-PRN-2 has had a paper feed fault and was offline from 2026-11-06. Until it is repaired, print to HB-PRN-1 only.

**East office**: one printer, named EA-PRN-1. The east office has no resident IT staff, so a fault there may wait until the visiting technician comes on Wednesday.

If you are not sure which printer you are near, check the name printed on the front of the machine or on the sticker on its side.

## Clearing a stuck job

A stuck job holds up everything behind it in the queue. To clear it, open your print queue on your computer, select the stuck job, and cancel it. Then send the document again to the same named printer. Choose the printer by its name, for example NO-PRN-1, rather than the first one that appears in the list. This avoids sending the job to an offline machine.

If the job still does not print, restart the printer by switching it off at the wall for ten seconds and switching it on again. Do not restart the printer while another person's job is printing.

## Printer offline

If a printer shows as offline on your computer, the most common causes are a paper jam, an empty tray or a machine that has not reconnected to the network. Look at the front display for a message, clear any jam, and refill the paper if needed. Then try the job again.

If the printer stays offline after that, print to another printer in the same office and open a ticket through the support form. Include the printer's name and what the display says. For the harbour office, use HB-PRN-1 while HB-PRN-2 is out of service.

## Toner

Toner is ordered by the desk, so you do not need to order it yourself. When a printer's display shows a low toner warning, open a ticket through the support form and name the printer. The desk orders the replacement and fits it when it arrives. A printer with an empty toner cartridge will not print, so raise the ticket before the printer runs out.

## When several people report problems

If several people report that a printer is failing, check the service status for printers before you do anything else. The desk may already know about the fault and be working on it. If the status shows a known problem, follow the workaround it gives and watch the ticket for updates.
