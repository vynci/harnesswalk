# Equipment Loans

Pebblebrook Studio keeps a small stock of spare laptops and headsets at the north office, so that people can keep working while their own equipment is being repaired, or while they are travelling or working from another office. This guide covers who can borrow equipment, how long a loan lasts, how to return it, and what happens if something is damaged.

## What can be borrowed

The loan stock has two kinds of item:

- Spare laptops. These are standard staff laptops with the studio software installed. They join the staff network and the VPN the same way your own laptop does.
- Headsets. These are for video calls and are especially useful in meeting rooms where the echo is a problem; see the video calls guide.

Loan stock is limited. If the item you want is out on loan, the desk can tell you when one is due back, and can reserve the next one for you through a ticket.

## Who can borrow and for how long

Any member of staff can borrow an item for up to two weeks. Loans are for work purposes only, and personal use is not covered. If you need an item for longer than two weeks, say so when you ask: the desk may extend the loan if no one else is waiting, but the extension is not automatic.

To borrow, open a ticket through the support form and say what you need, which office you will collect it from, and when you need it back. The desk answers new tickets within 4 working hours. Items are normally collected from the north office, and the desk arranges a handover at a time that suits you.

## Before you take a laptop

Check that the laptop is in working order when you collect it. The desk will set it up for you, so you should be able to sign in with your own account straight away. If the laptop does not sign in, or the screen or keyboard is faulty, tell the desk on the spot; it is easier to swap it then than later.

Do not change the laptop's security settings, and do not install software that is not part of the studio standard. If you need an application that is not installed, ask the desk to add it.

## Returning an item

Return loan items to the north office during desk hours, 08:00 to 18:00 on weekdays. Bring the laptop with its charger, or the headset with its case. The desk checks the item and marks the loan as closed. Please return items on the agreed date; if you need more time, ask before the date passes, not after.

Your files on the laptop are not kept between loans. Save your work to the file share before you return the laptop, so that it is covered by the nightly backup and can be restored if needed.

## Damage and loss

Look after the equipment as you would your own. Report damage or loss through the support form as soon as you notice it, and do not try to repair it yourself. The desk will tell you whether the item can be used again, and whether a replacement is needed. Lost equipment must be reported the same day, and the desk will advise on the next steps. Accidental damage should be reported honestly so that the stock can be kept in good order for everyone.

If a loan is returned late, the item may not be available for the next person who needs it. Returning on time keeps the stock available for colleagues who are waiting.
