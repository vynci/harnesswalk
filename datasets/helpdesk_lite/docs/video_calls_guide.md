# Video Calls in Meeting Rooms

Pebblebrook Studio has five meeting rooms with video call kits: Granite and Slate in the north office, Tide, Jetty and Lighthouse in the harbour office, and Orchard in the east office. This guide covers how to start a call in a room, how to fix the echo that sometimes appears, and when to report a problem.

## The rooms and their kits

Each room has a screen, a camera and a ceiling microphone, all connected to a single control panel by the door. The panel shows the room name, which should match the name on the booking. If the panel shows a different room name, do not use it; tell the desk, because the kit may be wired to the wrong place.

Rooms are booked in the shared calendar. The video kit does not need booking separately, but it can only host one call at a time, so check the calendar before you start.

## Starting a call

Connect your laptop to the screen with the cable on the panel, or join the call from the room's wireless display. Open your video app, choose the room's microphone and camera from the list of devices, and start or join the meeting. Test the sound with a short call before the meeting starts, so that you can fix any problems with time to spare.

If the room has no sound on the screen, check that the panel is set to the right input. If the camera is not showing, check it is not covered and that the room's kit is switched on at the panel.

## Fixing echo

Echo is the most common problem on video calls. It happens when the room's speaker sends sound into the microphone, and the far end hears their own voice a moment later. There are two quick fixes:

- Use a headset. A headset keeps the sound out of the room microphone, and it removes the echo for everyone on the call. Spare headsets can be borrowed from the north office (see the equipment loans guide).
- Mute the room speaker. If nobody has a headset, turn the room speaker down or off on the panel and let people speak through their laptop instead.

If the echo continues after one of these steps, leave the call, ask the other people in the room to mute their devices, and rejoin. If it still happens, open a ticket through the support form, naming the room and the time of the call.

## When to report a problem

Report a problem through the support form when a room's kit does not start, when the panel shows the wrong room name, when the camera or microphone is missing, or when echo comes back after the fixes above. Include the room name, the office, and what you tried. The desk answers new tickets within 4 working hours, and a technician will look at the room on their next visit if the fault cannot be fixed remotely.

Video call problems that affect every room at once may come from the network rather than the kit. Check the video calls status with the desk before assuming the room is faulty; the echo reported in two rooms in early November is an example of a shared issue that is handled centrally.

## Tips for smoother calls

Start calls a few minutes early, so the room is ready. Keep the room's door closed to reduce background noise, and close other applications that use the camera or microphone. For large calls, ask one person to host the call from the room and everyone else to join from their own device with the microphone muted until they speak.
