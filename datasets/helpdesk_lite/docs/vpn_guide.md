# VPN Guide

The VPN lets you reach the studio's file share and internal systems when you are working from home or away from the office. This guide explains when you need it, how to reconnect, and what to do when the VPN is down.

## When you need the VPN

You need the VPN to open the file share from home. Office staff on the office network can reach the file share directly, so you do not need the VPN while you are in the north, harbour or east office. The web apps that the studio uses, such as the time sheets and the project tracker, can be opened from anywhere without the VPN, provided you are signed in on the account portal.

## Connecting and reconnecting

Open the VPN app and sign in with your studio account. Once connected, the app reconnects by itself if your home connection drops for a few seconds, so you usually do not need to do anything. If the app shows that it is disconnected and does not reconnect after a minute, quit the app completely, reopen it and sign in again.

If the VPN is slow, first check your home internet connection with a web page that is not studio-related. If your own connection is slow too, the problem is at home and the desk cannot fix it. If only the studio's systems are slow, check the service status for the VPN before you raise a ticket.

## The gateway replacement on 2026-11-07

The VPN gateway was replaced on the morning of 2026-11-07. The outage started at 08:20 and was planned to last until 12:00. During that time, the VPN was unavailable for everyone. If you were working from home that morning, the file share was not reachable until the replacement finished.

After the replacement, the VPN should work normally. If you still have problems after 12:00 on that day, open a ticket through the support form and include the time of the problem.

## What to do during an outage

When the VPN is down, the studio's workaround is to use the office network or the web apps. If you are in an office, work on the office network and the file share is available as normal. If you are at home, use the web apps for anything that does not need the file share. Save your work locally and copy it to the file share once the VPN is back.

Do not try to access the file share through any other route. The studio only supports the VPN and the office network.

## When to open a ticket

Open a ticket through the support form if the VPN fails for you but works for your colleagues, if it still fails after you have quit and reopened the app, or if you see an error you do not recognise. The desk answers new tickets within 4 working hours. For a studio-wide outage, check the service status first, because the desk may already be working on it.
