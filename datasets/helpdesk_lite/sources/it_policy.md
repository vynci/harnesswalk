# Pebblebrook IT Policy

This policy explains how the IT desk works at Pebblebrook Studio, what it covers, and when you can expect help. It applies to all three offices: north, harbour and east.

## Desk hours

The IT desk is open from 08:00 to 18:00, Monday to Friday, at the north office. The harbour office desk runs shorter hours, from 09:00 to 17:00, Monday to Thursday. The east office has no resident IT staff. A visiting technician comes on Wednesdays, and the east office is covered from the north desk on the other days.

Outside these hours the desk does not answer requests. Please raise anything that can wait until the next desk opening.

## Urgent outages out of hours

Some faults cannot wait. If a service is down outside desk hours and your work has stopped, send an urgent request through the support form and mark it as an outage. The on-call technician picks up urgent outages outside desk hours. Use this route only for real outages, such as the file share being unreachable for the whole office or the VPN failing for everyone. A single slow printer or a forgotten password is not urgent.

## Raising a ticket

Every request to the desk should be opened as a ticket through the support form. A ticket gives the desk a record of what you asked for, lets other people see progress, and means nothing is lost when someone is away. Do not rely on a chat message or a hallway conversation for anything that needs follow-up.

When you open a ticket, include a short subject, the office you are in, and what you were doing when the problem started. If the problem affects a specific printer, meeting room or service, name it.

## Answer times

The desk answers every new ticket within 4 working hours. A working hour counts only while the desk is open for your office. If a ticket has not been answered within that time, reply on the same ticket instead of opening a new one, so the history stays in one place.

## Ticket statuses

A ticket has one of four statuses at any time:

- **open**: the desk has received the ticket and has not yet started work on it.
- **in_progress**: a technician is working on the ticket.
- **waiting_on_you**: the desk needs something from you, such as a reply, a room name or a test result. Check your ticket when this status appears.
- **resolved**: the desk considers the problem fixed. If it is not, reply on the ticket and it will be reopened.

## The five day rule

A ticket marked waiting_on_you closes automatically after 5 working days without a reply from you. This keeps the queue clear. If you still need help after a ticket closes, open a new ticket and mention the old one by its number.

## Who to contact

For anything about a ticket already open, reply on that ticket. For a new request, use the support form. The desk never asks for passwords by chat, email or phone, so treat any such request as suspicious and report it through the support form.
