# Password reset

1. Never ask for the old or the new password, and never type one.
2. Send them to the self-service account portal: it resets a password in about 5 minutes.
3. A locked account unlocks by itself after 15 minutes.
4. If the portal fails, open a ticket through the support form; the desk answers within 4 working hours.
