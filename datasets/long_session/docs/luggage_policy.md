# Ferry Luggage, Bicycles and Pets

This page explains what a foot passenger, a car traveller or a dog owner may bring on the Carrow Ferry Company crossings run for Fernhollow Journeys. The rules apply to the MIR-CAR, CAR-MIR and ELL-CAR routes alike.

## Luggage for foot passengers

Every foot passenger may take two bags of up to 23 kg each without charge. A bag over 23 kg is not carried free and is not accepted for the crossing, so repack before you travel. Hand luggage that you keep with you in the cabin or on deck is not counted as one of the two bags, provided it can be carried by one person without help.

## Luggage in a car

Travellers who cross by car have no luggage limit. Everything that fits inside the vehicle may travel with it, subject to the normal safety rules of the ferry crew. Roof boxes and trailers must be declared at the ticket desk when the crossing is booked.

## Bicycles

Bicycles cost 6.00 each and must be booked in advance. A bicycle cannot be taken on the ferry on the day without a booking, because the crew needs to plan the deck space. Bicycles are carried on the car deck and are secured by the crew.

## Dogs and other pets

Dogs travel free. They may stay on the deck with their owner or in a car on the car deck. Dogs are not allowed in the passenger cabins, including the cabin booked for the overnight sailing. Assistance dogs are treated in the same way as other dogs for the crossing itself.

## Worked example

A couple from Mirelake plan a day crossing from MIR-CAR at 12:30. They have three large suitcases, a backpack each and one bicycle. As foot passengers they may take two bags each free, so they hold four bags free of charge. The third suitcase weighs 25 kg, so it must be repacked or left out. The bicycle must be booked at 6.00 before the crossing, which adds 6.00 to the trip. Their dog travels free on deck with them.

## Questions and answers

**Can I take a bag heavier than 23 kg?** No, not as one of your free bags. Repack it so that each bag is under the limit.

**Do I have to book a bicycle in advance?** Yes. The 6.00 charge is paid at booking, and an unbooked bicycle may be refused.

**Can my dog come into the cabin on the overnight sailing?** No. Dogs stay on deck or in a car. Ask the ticket desk about a place for your dog before you board.

**Is there a luggage limit for travellers in a car?** No. The vehicle may carry whatever fits, but roof boxes and trailers must be declared.
