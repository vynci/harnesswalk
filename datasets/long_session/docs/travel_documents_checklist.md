# Travel Documents Checklist

Use this checklist before you travel with Fernhollow Journeys. Keeping the right papers ready means the crossing and the hotel check-in go smoothly. Questions can be sent to the desk, which is staffed 08:00 to 18:00, Monday to Friday.

## Photo identity for adults

Every adult on the Carrow ferry needs a photo identity document. A passport, a national identity card or a driving licence is accepted. Check that the document is in date for the whole stay and bring it on the day of travel. The ferry staff check photo identity at the boarding gate, and a traveller without it cannot board, even if the booking is confirmed.

## Children

Children under 5 travel free on the ferry and need no ticket of their own. Children aged 5 to 15 pay half the foot fare. Birth certificate copies are not needed for travel on the Carrow ferry. Each child should, however, have a photo identity document if the family wants the simplest check at the gate. Ask the desk if you are unsure which document applies to a child on your trip.

## Booking confirmations

Keep the following confirmations with your travel papers:

- The trip reference, which starts with TR- and is followed by four digits.
- The ferry booking for each crossing, showing the route, the departure time and the number of foot passengers, cars or bicycles.
- The hotel confirmation for each partner hotel on the trip.
- The payment record showing the 20 percent deposit has been received. The balance is due 14 days before departure, and the trip is only confirmed once the deposit is paid.

## Travel insurance

Travel insurance is your own choice. Fernhollow Journeys does not sell insurance and cannot advise on which policy to buy. The desk only notes on the trip file whether you have a policy, so that the booking records are complete. If you are arranging cover yourself, check that it covers the crossing and the hotel terms for your dates.

## Before you leave home

- Check that every adult's photo identity is in date and matches the names on the booking.
- Print or save the trip reference and the ferry times.
- Note the hotel's check-in time and the nearest harbour for your arrival.
- Pack the luggage allowance for the ferry: two bags of up to 23 kg for each foot passenger.
- If the trip falls on a winter closure date, such as 25 December 2026, check the timetable with the desk before you travel.
