# Isle of Carrow Visitor Guide

The Isle of Carrow is a small island with harbour villages, open moorland and a quiet pace.
There is no rail on the island; most visitors arrive by the Carrow Ferry Company from Mirelake
harbour or, in winter, from Port Ellery on Friday to Monday.

## Getting there by ferry

From Mirelake harbour (MIR-CAR) the winter sailings leave at 08:15, 12:30, 16:45 and 23:30.
The day crossing takes 1 h 40 min. The 23:30 overnight sailing arrives at 01:10 and includes a
cabin for 30.00 extra. From Port Ellery (ELL-CAR), the winter sailings leave at 09:30 and
15:00, Friday to Monday only, and the crossing takes 2 h 55 min. Foot passengers pay 18.00 from
Mirelake and 26.00 from Port Ellery; cars pay 64.00 and 88.00 respectively.

## Getting around

A local bus meets every daytime ferry at the harbour and runs along the coast road to the
villages. The bus is low floor, so it suits travellers with pushchairs and mobility aids. There
are no taxis on the island, so ask the desk to arrange a pickup from a village if you need one
after the last bus.

## Walks

The coastal path runs for about nine kilometres from the harbour to the north point and takes
around three hours at an easy pace. The moorland walks start from the village of Kell and are
best done in daylight during the short winter days. Carry a torch and a map even on the short
routes, because mist can come in quickly.

## Festivals and events

The harbour festival takes place on 12 December 2026. To help visitors get home afterwards, the
Carrow Ferry Company adds an extra 19:00 sailing from Mirelake that day. No ferries run on
25 December 2026 at all.

## Where to eat

The harbour has three cafes that open for breakfast and a fish kitchen that serves until
21:00. Most villages have one pub with a kitchen; the pub in Kell serves vegetarian dishes and
can prepare a nut-free plate if you ask in advance.

## Where to stay

The Carrow Harbour Inn (HT-12) sits on the harbour front. It includes breakfast, has one
accessible room and offers no parking, so car travellers should use the harbour car park. Check
in from 15:00.
