# Port Ellery Guide

Port Ellery is a coastal town with a long sand beach and a small harbour. It is reached by regional rail from Mirelake and is the start of the winter crossing to the Isle of Carrow on Friday to Monday. Most visitors come in summer; in winter the town is quiet and the beach huts are closed.

## Getting there

Trains run from Mirelake to Port Ellery several times a day. The journey takes just over an hour. From the station the Ellery Sands Guesthouse is a short walk along the front.

## Where to stay

The Ellery Sands Guesthouse (HT-30) sits on the seafront. Check-in is from 15:30 and breakfast is included, with a nut-free option available on request. Parking is free, and the guesthouse has 2 accessible rooms. Cancellation is free until 72 hours before arrival.

## The beach and the huts

The beach huts along the front close from 1 November to 31 March, so they are not available during winter. The beach itself stays open, and the cliff path behind the town is walkable all year. In winter, travellers should check weather notices for the sea wall.

## The winter ferry to Carrow

From 1 November to 31 March the Port Ellery to Carrow route runs only on Friday to Monday. The ELL-CAR crossing departs at 09:30 and 15:00 and takes 2 hours and 55 minutes. Foot passengers pay 26.00 and cars pay 88.00 from Port Ellery. On days the route does not run, travellers should take the rail connection to Mirelake and the MIR-CAR crossing instead.

## Worked example: a weekend break and the ferry

A traveller stays at the guesthouse from Friday to Monday and takes the 09:30 ELL-CAR sailing on Monday morning. The crossing takes just under three hours, so an arrival on the Isle of Carrow is around midday. Because the route runs on Monday, the traveller does not need to change their plan.

## Questions and answers

**Are the beach huts open in winter?** No, they close from 1 November to 31 March.

**Which days does the Carrow ferry run from here?** Friday to Monday only in winter.

**Does the guesthouse have accessible rooms?** Yes, two.

**How long is the crossing to Carrow?** 2 hours and 55 minutes.
