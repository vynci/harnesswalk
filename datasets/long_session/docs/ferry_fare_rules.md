# Carrow Ferry Fares and Ticket Rules

The Carrow Ferry Company runs three routes. This sheet lists the fares, the children's rules, the overnight supplement and the rules for changing a ticket. Fares are for the winter timetable from 1 November 2026 to 31 March 2027.

## Routes and fares

- **MIR-CAR**, Mirelake harbour to the Isle of Carrow: foot passenger 18.00, car 64.00. Crossing takes 1 hour 40 minutes.
- **CAR-MIR**, the Isle of Carrow back to Mirelake: foot passenger 18.00, car 64.00. Crossing takes 1 hour 40 minutes.
- **ELL-CAR**, Port Ellery to the Isle of Carrow: foot passenger 26.00, car 88.00. Crossing takes 2 hours 55 minutes, and the route runs Friday to Monday only in winter.

## Children

Children under 5 travel free. Children aged 5 to 15 pay half the foot fare on the route they travel. Children do not need a birth certificate copy for the ferry.

## The overnight sailing

The MIR-CAR sailing at 23:30 is overnight and arrives at 01:10. A cabin for the night costs 30.00 extra per cabin. Cabins are not available on the day sailings.

## Changing a ticket

A ticket can be changed free of charge up to 2 hours before departure. After that point a change costs 5.00 per ticket. Changes are made through the desk within one working day for trips booked through Fernhollow Journeys; the assistant can only read the booking.

## Worked example: a family of four

A family of two adults and two children, aged 6 and 9, takes the 12:30 MIR-CAR crossing with a car. The adults pay 18.00 each. The 6-year-old pays half the foot fare, 9.00. The 9-year-old pays half the foot fare, 9.00. The car costs 64.00 once. Total foot fares come to 54.00, plus 64.00 for the car, a total of 118.00. If the family changes the sailing 3 hours before departure, the change is free. If it changes 1 hour before, the change costs 5.00 per ticket.

## Questions and answers

**Do children pay full fare?** Under 5s travel free; 5 to 15s pay half the foot fare.

**What does the overnight cabin cost?** 30.00 extra per cabin, on the 23:30 MIR-CAR sailing only.

**Can I change my sailing?** Yes, free up to 2 hours before departure; after that a change costs 5.00.

**Is the Port Ellery crossing more expensive?** Yes: 26.00 for a foot passenger and 88.00 for a car.
