# Accessible Travel Guide

Fernhollow Journeys wants every traveller to reach their destination comfortably. This guide sets out what each partner hotel, the ferries and the local transport can offer, and what to tell the desk when you book. The desk is staffed 08:00 to 18:00, Monday to Friday.

## Accessible rooms at partner hotels

Each partner hotel keeps a limited number of rooms with step-free access and roll-in showers. Rooms are allocated in the order that bookings are confirmed, so ask early.

- Mirelake Quay Hotel (HT-15): 4 accessible rooms.
- Ellery Sands Guesthouse (HT-30): 2 accessible rooms.
- Carrow Harbour Inn (HT-12): 1 accessible room.
- Brannock Lodge (HT-21): no accessible rooms. Travellers who need step-free access should choose another hotel for a Brannock Highlands trip, or ask the desk to look at a nearby alternative.

Check-in times vary by hotel (14:00 at Mirelake Quay Hotel, 15:00 at Carrow Harbour Inn, 15:30 at Ellery Sands Guesthouse and 16:00 at Brannock Lodge), and an early arrival can be discussed with the hotel if you tell the desk in advance.

## Ferry access

The Carrow ferry has a lift to the passenger deck, so foot passengers who cannot use the stairs can reach the main lounge. There are two wheelchair spaces on each sailing. Because the spaces are limited, you must book them at least 48 hours before the sailing. Tell the desk when you book, and the space will be held on the sailing you choose.

Wheelchair users travelling by car can use the car deck; a car with a blue badge does not pay extra for the crossing. Let the desk know about any mobility aid that travels with you so that it can be stowed safely.

## Local transport on the Isle of Carrow

The harbour bus that meets each daytime ferry is low floor, so a wheelchair or a folding walker can board without a ramp. The bus runs to the main hotels on the island. Ask the desk for the current bus times, as they follow the ferry timetable and change with the winter season.

## What to tell the desk when booking

To plan the trip well, tell the desk the following when you book or ask for a change:

1. Whether any traveller needs a step-free room, and how many rooms are needed.
2. Whether a wheelchair or mobility aid travels with you, and its size and weight.
3. Whether you need a wheelchair space on the ferry, so the space can be booked 48 hours ahead.
4. Any dietary need that affects breakfast. Carrow Harbour Inn offers nut-free breakfast on request, and Ellery Sands Guesthouse does the same. Mirelake Quay Hotel has a separate kitchen area for nut-free meals.
5. Any other need the desk should keep on the booking, such as a quieter room or a lift-accessible route from the harbour.

The desk records these needs on the trip file, so you do not have to repeat them at every stage. If a detail changes, let the desk know within one working day so that the booking can be updated.
