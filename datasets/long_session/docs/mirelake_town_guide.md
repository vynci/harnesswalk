# Mirelake Town Guide

Mirelake is a lakeside town on the northern shore of the regional lake. It is the usual starting point for trips to the Isle of Carrow, because the Carrow Ferry Company runs its main crossings from Mirelake harbour, and it is the rail hub for the Brannock Highlands and Port Ellery.

## Getting around

The regional rail station sits a short walk from the town centre and the lake front. Trains link Mirelake with the Brannock Highlands and with Port Ellery. From the station it is about ten minutes on foot to the ferry harbour, where the MIR-CAR crossings to the Isle of Carrow leave. Taxis wait outside the station on arrival and are the easiest option with heavy luggage.

## Where to stay

The Mirelake Quay Hotel (HT-15) sits on the lake front, five minutes from the harbour. It has 4 accessible rooms, a separate nut-free kitchen area for breakfast on request, and an included breakfast for all guests. Parking is available at 12.00 per night, and the hotel asks for free cancellation until 24 hours before arrival.

## The lake and the winter market

Lake walks are signposted from the harbour in both directions. The flat path round the north shore is suitable for pushchairs and wheelchairs for most of its length. In December the town holds a winter market on Saturdays, with stalls along the quay from late morning until dusk. It is a good place to spend the time between a rail arrival and an afternoon ferry.

## Worked example: a day between two trains

A traveller arrives on the 09:05 train from the Brannock Highlands and takes the 12:30 MIR-CAR ferry the same day. Allowing an hour to reach the harbour and check in, there is time for a short lake walk after the station, but not a visit to the market. Booking the ferry for the 16:45 sailing instead gives time for a lunch in town and a stroll along the quay.

## Questions and answers

**Can I leave luggage at the hotel before a ferry?** Ask the Mirelake Quay Hotel at check-in; the desk cannot hold luggage on the traveller's behalf.

**How far is the harbour from the station?** About ten minutes on foot, or a short taxi ride with luggage.

**Is the lake path accessible?** The north shore path is level and suitable for wheelchairs for most of its length.

**When is the market open?** Saturdays in December, from late morning until dusk.
