# Try these: Fernhollow Journeys

Planning chats here run long. Paste a few long notes into one session and watch the context
builder's session slice fill up: past its threshold the oldest turns fold into a running
summary and only the last four stay verbatim. What was said first survives; a detail buried
deep in old notes may not. Ask it to remember a preference, then ask again in a new session.

1. What is the status of trip TR-4471?
   *One read tool, `trip_lookup`.*
2. When do the ferries from Mirelake to the Isle of Carrow leave?
   *`ferry_timetable` for MIR-CAR.*
3. Please remember that I always want an aisle seat on trains.
   *`remember`: the memory writer writes the fact after the reply.*
4. Which seat do I like on trains? (in a new session)
   *Semantic memory: the saved preference reaches the context.*
5. What is the cancellation policy at the Carrow Harbour Inn (HT-12)?
   *`hotel_info`.*
6. What are the luggage limits on the Carrow ferry?
   *Knowledge: a cited answer.*
7. What happened with my last trip, TR-4390?
   *Episodic memory: a dated event about the trip.*
