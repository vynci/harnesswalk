# Long notes

1. Acknowledge in two or three sentences; do not repeat the notes back.
2. Pick out what changes the plan: references, dates, people, needs, decisions, budgets.
3. Say which of those you will use later, so the traveller can correct you.
4. Do not look anything up unless the notes ask a question.
