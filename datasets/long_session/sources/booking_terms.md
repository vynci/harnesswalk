# Fernhollow Journeys Booking Terms

These terms set out how a trip booked through Fernhollow Journeys is paid for, changed and cancelled. They sit alongside the terms of each partner hotel and of the Carrow Ferry Company, and where they overlap the stricter rule applies to the traveller.

## Deposit and confirmation

A trip is confirmed once a deposit of 20 percent of the trip cost has been paid. Until the deposit arrives the trip is held, but it is not confirmed and no hotel room is guaranteed. The balance is due 14 days before the departure date.

## Cancellation bands

The refund you receive depends on how long before departure you cancel.

- More than 30 days before departure: everything is refunded except the 20 percent deposit.
- Between 30 and 14 days before departure: 50 percent of the trip cost is refunded.
- Fewer than 14 days before departure: nothing is refunded.

Hotel cancellation terms apply on top of these bands. A partner hotel may charge for a room cancelled inside its own deadline even when the Fernhollow band would allow a refund, so check the hotel's terms when you book.

## Changes

Changes to dates, travellers, hotels and crossings are made by the desk within one working day of your request. The planning assistant can read your booking and list the changes you have asked for, but it cannot make them itself. Ferry ticket changes are governed separately: they are free up to 2 hours before departure and cost 5.00 after that.

## Worked example

A family cancels a trip to the Isle of Carrow with a departure date 21 days away. The trip cost is 1,200.00 and the deposit paid was 240.00. The cancellation falls in the 30 to 14 day band, so 50 percent of the trip cost is refunded: 600.00. The Carrow Harbour Inn also applies its own rule and keeps the first night, 150.00, so the family receives 450.00 back.

## Questions and answers

**Is my trip confirmed before I pay the deposit?** No. The trip is held, but it is confirmed only once the 20 percent deposit has been paid.

**When is the balance due?** 14 days before departure.

**Can the assistant change my booking?** It can read your booking and list the changes you asked for. The desk makes the changes within one working day.

**Does the hotel's rule override the Fernhollow band?** Both apply. Where they differ, the stricter rule applies to you, so read the hotel's terms as well.
