# Handling a cargo claim question

1. If the question mentions a shipment, call `track` to confirm the shipment exists and its contract code.
2. Search the knowledge base for the cargo claims procedure and the carrier contract summary for that contract code.
3. State the claim window (21 days from delivery or the expected delivery date), the liability per kilogram on that contract and the evidence needed, citing the passages as [1], [2].
4. Do not promise a payout. Say the claims team reviews each claim and that the operations desk can log it now.
5. If the shipper has not delivered yet, say the claim window has not started.
