# Hub Directory

## Overview

Tallowgate Freight runs six hubs. Each has a three-letter code used in every system, a daily cutoff time and a main role. Freight that arrives after a hub's cutoff is sorted the next day. The utilisation target for all hubs is under 80 percent; above 90 percent a hub is marked congested and stops accepting reroutes. This directory is the reference for hub questions and for the reroute checks in the Reroute SOP.

## KRN: Kernmoor Central

Kernmoor Central sits in the Kernmoor industrial district and is the largest hub in the network. It handles linehaul for most long lanes and has the biggest cross-dock floor. The daily cutoff is 17:30. Utilisation normally runs near 60 to 65 percent. KRN is approved for temperature-controlled loads and accepts reroutes when open.

## MAV: Mavisgate Cross-dock

Mavisgate is in the Mavisgate Interchange district beside the ring road. It is a fast cross-dock built for pallet freight that changes trailers within hours. The daily cutoff is 18:00. It often acts as the alternate hub for lanes through Kernmoor. It accepts reroutes when open.

## HLM: Holmquay Port Terminal

Holmquay is in the Harbour District and handles freight arriving by sea, plus export consolidation. The daily cutoff is 16:00, the earliest in the network. The terminal is currently congested, with utilisation above 90 percent, because of a berth queue, and it does not accept reroutes while that continues. Shipments held there are reviewed at every shift change.

## TRB: Tarnwick Gate Depot

Tarnwick Gate is in the Tarnwick Gate business park and serves the south-west lanes. It is a smaller depot with spare capacity, normally under 50 percent utilisation. The daily cutoff is 19:00. It accepts reroutes when open and is the usual alternate for lanes through Holmquay.

## WYN: Wyncliff Rail Yard

Wyncliff is in the Wyncliff Sidings district and links road freight with the rail network. The daily cutoff is 15:00. The yard is closed until further notice because of yard works, and it does not accept reroutes or new arrivals while closed. Shipments already held there are being moved out under a recovery plan, and reroute fees are waived under the Reroute SOP for those shipments.

## ORS: Orsley Park Sorting Centre

Orsley Park is in the Orsley Park district on the northern edge of the network. It is the main sorting centre for parcels and small consignments and runs the latest cutoff at 20:00. Utilisation normally sits in the high 60s. It accepts reroutes when open and is approved for fragile and temperature-controlled loads.

## Using the directory

For live status, utilisation and the accepts-reroutes flag, always check the capacity tool rather than relying on the figures here, because they change during the day. The cutoffs and roles in this directory change only when the network schedule is reissued.
