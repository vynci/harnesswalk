# Carrier Contract Summary CC-17

This summary describes contract CC-17, which Tallowgate Freight holds with the carrier Greywater Haulage. It is written for the operations desk and for account managers who need to answer shipper questions without opening the full agreement. Where this page and the signed contract differ, the signed contract wins.

## Parties and scope

Contract CC-17 covers road linehaul between the Tallowgate hubs and their home-region delivery points. Greywater Haulage operates the trunk legs and the final delivery vans. Tallowgate Freight remains the shipper's single point of contact for every consignment moved under this contract. Shippers do not need to speak to the carrier directly, and the operations desk should not give out carrier driver contact details.

Shipments currently on CC-17 include TG-604118, TG-604305 and TG-604733. The contract code appears on every tracking record, so check it before quoting terms.

## Service level

CC-17 is a next-day service within the home region. A consignment booked and handed over before the pickup cutoff is delivered on the next working day, between 8:00 and 18:00, unless the shipper has agreed a narrower window. Saturday delivery is not included. Consignments that cross the regional boundary are treated as two-day moves and are quoted separately.

Next-day service is a target, not a guarantee of an hour. The delivery window is confirmed through the `eta` record once the consignment has left its first hub. When the confidence on that record is `low`, the window may move and the desk should say so.

## Pickup cutoff

The pickup cutoff for CC-17 is 16:00 local time. Freight that is ready after 16:00 is collected the next working day and the service clock starts then. Hub arrival cutoffs are a separate matter: each hub has its own daily cutoff, listed in the hub directory, and freight reaching a hub after that time is sorted the following day.

## Liability

Liability under CC-17 is limited to 8.50 per kilogram of the damaged, lost or short-delivered goods, measured on the gross weight shown on the delivery note. The limit applies per consignment and covers loss and damage in transit. It does not cover consequential loss such as missed sales, idle labour or contract penalties. A shipper who needs higher cover should ask their account manager about declared-value insurance before the shipment is booked, not afterwards.

## Claim window

A claim under CC-17 must be lodged within 21 days of delivery. For goods that never arrive, the 21 days run from the expected delivery date. Claims lodged after the window are normally declined. The full process, the evidence required and the review time are set out in the cargo claims procedure. The desk can log a claim at any time, including outside account manager hours.

## Alternate hub arrangements

Every CC-17 consignment has an alternate hub recorded on its tracking record. For TG-604118 the alternate hub is Mavisgate (MAV), for TG-604305 it is Orsley Park (ORS) and for TG-604733 it is Holmquay (HLM). A reroute may only go through the alternate hub recorded for that shipment, and only while that hub is open and accepting reroutes. Holmquay is frequently congested and often does not accept reroutes, so a CC-17 shipment whose alternate is HLM may have no available reroute at busy times. Every reroute needs a human approval before it takes effect.

## What the desk should say

Quote the next-day target and the 16:00 pickup cutoff, state the liability figure and the 21-day claim window, and never promise a delivery time that the `eta` record does not support. If a shipper asks to change carrier, refer them to their account manager during weekday hours.
