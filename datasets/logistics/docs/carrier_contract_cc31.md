# Carrier Contract Summary CC-31

This summary describes contract CC-31, which Tallowgate Freight holds with the carrier Ostrander Freightways. It is meant for the operations desk and for account managers answering routine shipper questions. The signed contract overrides this page if they disagree.

## Parties and scope

CC-31 is the economy contract. Ostrander Freightways moves consignments between Tallowgate hubs on scheduled trunk runs and delivers to points across the home region and the outer districts. Tallowgate Freight is the shipper's only point of contact and handles every query. Shipments currently on CC-31 include TG-604412 and TG-604677. The contract code is on every tracking record, so check it before quoting terms.

## Service level

CC-31 is an economy three-day service. A consignment handed over before the pickup cutoff is delivered within three working days. Delivery windows are wider than on the faster contracts, typically 8:00 to 18:00, and a narrower window is not available. Weekend delivery is not included. Because the trunk runs are scheduled rather than on demand, a cancelled linehaul can add a full day to a consignment. The desk should check the `eta` record and quote its confidence, especially when the delay reason is a missed or cancelled run.

## Weight limits

A single piece on CC-31 may weigh at most 1,000 kilograms. Pieces above that limit are refused at pickup and must be split or moved on a different contract. The limit applies to the gross weight of the piece including pallet and packaging. Standard pallet height and packaging rules also apply, as set out in the packaging and pallet standards. If a shipper asks to move a heavier piece, refer them to their account manager during weekday hours.

## Liability

Liability under CC-31 is limited to 6.00 per kilogram of the damaged, lost or short-delivered goods, using the gross weight on the delivery note. This is the lowest of the three Tallowgate contracts and reflects the economy price. It does not cover consequential loss. Shippers moving valuable goods on CC-31 should be told clearly that the standard cover is modest.

## Optional insured cover

Shippers can upgrade a consignment to insured cover before it is booked. Insured cover replaces the 6.00 per kilogram limit with the declared value of the goods, up to the maximum on the shipper's rate card, for an additional percentage charge. It cannot be added after the consignment has been handed over. The desk should not quote the exact percentage and should refer the shipper to their account manager.

## Claim window

A claim under CC-31 must be lodged within 21 days of delivery, or from the expected delivery date for goods that do not arrive. The cargo claims procedure sets out the evidence needed and the review time. The desk can log a claim at any hour.

## Alternate hub arrangements

Each CC-31 consignment records an alternate hub. For TG-604412 the alternate is Kernmoor Central (KRN) and for TG-604677 it is Wyncliff (WYN). Wyncliff is currently closed for yard works, so TG-604677 has no available reroute at present. A reroute is only possible when the alternate hub is open and accepts reroutes, and it always needs a human approval before it takes effect.

## What the desk should say

Quote the three-day economy service, the 1,000 kilogram piece limit, the 6.00 per kilogram liability and the 21-day claim window.
