# Holiday Operating Schedule

This notice sets out how Tallowgate Freight runs its hubs, collections and support desks over the 2026 year-end holidays. Please plan consignments around it, because several hubs and the account management team run reduced hours. Dates below are for 2026 unless stated otherwise.

## Summary of key dates

All six hubs (KRN, MAV, HLM, TRB, WYN and ORS) are closed on 2026-12-25. No linehaul departs, no freight is sorted and no deliveries are made at hub gates on that day. Collections and deliveries on 2026-12-24 and 2026-12-26 run on a reduced pattern, described in the sections below. Normal operations resume on 2026-12-27, except where a hub has an unrelated closure notice.

## Hub hours

On 2026-12-24 every hub keeps its normal opening hours for inbound freight, but the daily cutoff times in the Hub Directory are brought forward by two hours. Freight that misses the earlier cutoff is held and sorted when the hub reopens. On 2026-12-25 the gates are shut for the full day. On 2026-12-26 hubs open at 08:00 and close at 16:00, and cutoffs are set to 14:00 for every hub.

Please remember that the Wyncliff Rail Yard (WYN) may still be closed for yard works, and that any shipment held there is handled under the reroute and delay procedures rather than this schedule.

## Collections and pickups

Last pickup on 2026-12-24 is 12:00 at every origin. Pickups booked for the afternoon of 2026-12-24 are moved automatically to 2026-12-27 and the shipper is told through the customer portal. No pickups are made on 2026-12-25. On 2026-12-26 pickups are limited to pre-booked consignments on contract lanes, and same-day booking is not available.

## Linehaul

Linehaul runs on a limited timetable on 2026-12-26. Only the trunk routes between the three busiest hubs depart that day, so shipments on other routes will show a longer ETA with low or medium confidence. Shippers can expect delay reasons such as no_linehaul_slot on tracking and ETA results. These delays caused only by the holiday timetable do not count toward the 24-hour escalation rule unless the shipment is still stationary on 2026-12-28.

## Operations desk

The operations desk runs 24/7 throughout the holidays, including 2026-12-25. It can track shipments, check ETAs, report hub status and log claims. Because hubs are closed on 2026-12-25, a reroute requested that day is queued for human approval and takes effect when the alternate hub reopens. The desk will tell you this at the time of the request.

## Account managers

Account managers are closed on 2026-12-25 and 2026-12-26. On other days they work their normal hours, Monday to Friday, 7:00 to 19:00. Anything that needs an account manager during the closure, such as a contract change or a goodwill decision on a claim, is logged and picked up on the next working day. Urgent operational matters should be raised with the operations desk, which can escalate to the on-call duty manager.

## Planning tips

Book collections for the holiday weeks early, and avoid scheduling delivery appointments for 2026-12-25 or 2026-12-26. Check that the consignee can accept freight on the dates you choose. Remember that the peak season surcharge is in force until 2026-12-24, as set out in the Peak Season Surcharge Notice.
