# Peak Season Surcharge Notice

## Summary

From 2026-11-16 to 2026-12-24 inclusive, Tallowgate Freight applies a **peak season surcharge of 4.5 percent** of the freight charge. The surcharge covers extra linehaul capacity, additional sorting shifts and the higher cost of holiday-period staffing across our six hubs. This notice applies to all shippers and to contracts CC-17, CC-22 and CC-31.

## What the surcharge is applied to

The surcharge is calculated on the base freight charge only. It is not charged on fuel adjustments already shown separately, on storage fees, on customs duties or on insurance. It appears as its own line on the invoice, labelled "peak season surcharge".

For example, a consignment with a freight charge of 2,000.00 carries a surcharge of 90.00, bringing the freight line to 2,090.00 before other charges.

## Which shipments are covered

The surcharge applies to every domestic lane and every cross-border lane, including the lanes between Northreach, Eastmere and the Southern Reaches. The date that counts is the pickup date. A shipment collected on 2026-11-15 is not charged the surcharge even if it is delivered after 2026-11-16. A shipment collected on 2026-12-24 is charged even if it is delivered after the holidays.

## Exemptions

Contracts on fixed-rate schedules are exempt, because the peak charge is already built into their rates. Contract CC-17 and contract CC-22 are fixed-rate contracts and are exempt. **There is no exemption for CC-31.** CC-31 shipments carry the 4.5 percent surcharge for the whole period. Reroutes within the same contract do not add a second surcharge, but a reroute that changes the lane may change the base freight charge.

## Booking cut-off recommendation

To arrive before the holidays, we recommend booking by **2026-12-10**. Bookings made after that date are accepted, but capacity is limited and we cannot guarantee delivery before 2026-12-24. Pickups booked after the holiday cut-off may be pushed to the next available linehaul. The holiday operating schedule lists the hub closures.

## Practical tips

- Book early and give accurate pallet counts so we can allocate space.
- Meet the packaging and pallet standards, since rejected pallets cause costly delays at peak times.
- Send customs paperwork at booking for cross-border lanes.
- Check the hub cutoffs in the hub directory. Freight arriving after cutoff is sorted the next day, and queues are longer in peak weeks.

## Disputes

If you believe the surcharge has been applied in error, raise it with your account manager within 14 days of the invoice date. Account managers are available Monday to Friday, 7:00 to 19:00. The operations desk can log the dispute at any time, 24/7.

## Changes to this notice

Tallowgate Freight may extend the period if capacity stays tight, and will give at least 7 days of notice on the customer portal.
