# Customs Paperwork Checklist

## Who this is for

This checklist is for shippers sending freight across a border on a Tallowgate Freight lane. The lanes connect three regions: Northreach, Eastmere and the Southern Reaches. Domestic movements inside one region need none of the documents below. The checklist applies under every carrier contract (CC-17, CC-22 and CC-31).

## Required documents

For every cross-border consignment, supply all of the following:

1. **Commercial invoice.** It must name the seller and the buyer, describe the goods in plain words, give the quantity and the unit and total value, and state the currency and the terms of sale.
2. **Packing list.** It lists each pallet or package with its dimensions, weight and contents. The total must match the booking.
3. **Origin declaration.** It states the region where the goods were made or substantially transformed. This decides whether a lower duty rate can apply.
4. **Consignee tax registration.** The registration reference of the receiver in the destination region.

Add the shipment id (for example TG-604305) to every page so the documents can be matched to the cargo.

## Lead time

Customs filings need **2 working days** before the shipment reaches the border hub. Send documents at booking, and no later than 2 working days before pickup if the lane is a rush lane. Documents received later than that may cause a missed slot, and the shipment will wait for the next one.

## Document quality

- Names and addresses must match the booking exactly.
- Values must be stated in a single currency per invoice.
- Descriptions such as "parts" or "goods" are not accepted. Use a specific description such as "ceramic floor tiles, glazed".
- Photocopies are acceptable only when legible. Scanned PDFs are preferred.

## Restricted and special goods

Some goods need extra permits, such as food, plant material, chemicals and electronics with radio parts. Dangerous goods follow the dangerous goods acceptance policy as well. Ask the operations desk before booking if you are unsure.

## Incomplete paperwork

If any required document is missing, unreadable or inconsistent, the shipment status becomes **held** at the border hub. A held shipment does not move until the paperwork is corrected. Storage at the hub is free for the first 48 hours and charged after that at the rate in your contract. The desk tells the shipper by the preferred contact route within two hours of the hold, and an account manager follows up in office hours.

## Duties and taxes

Duties and taxes are paid by the party named at booking. If the receiver is named and refuses payment, the shipper remains responsible. Tallowgate Freight does not advance duties unless the account has a deferred payment arrangement.

## Changing documents after filing

A correction after filing needs a fresh filing and is treated as a new lead time. Tell the operations desk the same day so the hold, if any, can be cleared quickly.

## Contact

The operations desk is available 24/7. Account managers are available Monday to Friday, 7:00 to 19:00.
