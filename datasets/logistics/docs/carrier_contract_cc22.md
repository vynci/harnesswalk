# Carrier Contract Summary CC-22

This summary describes contract CC-22, which Tallowgate Freight holds with the carrier Bellmoor Linehaul. It exists so the operations desk can answer common shipper questions quickly. The signed contract is the final authority if there is any difference.

## Parties and scope

CC-22 covers linehaul between Tallowgate hubs and delivery points across the home region and the neighbouring districts. Bellmoor Linehaul runs the trunk movements and the temperature-controlled fleet. Tallowgate Freight stays the shipper's only point of contact. Shipments currently on CC-22 include TG-604227 and TG-604590. The contract code is shown on each tracking record, so confirm it before quoting terms.

## Service level

CC-22 is a two-day service. A consignment handed over before the pickup cutoff is delivered within two working days, with a delivery window of 8:00 to 18:00 unless a narrower window was agreed at booking. Weekend delivery is not included. Because the service is built around trunk consolidation, consignments spend more time inside hubs than they do on next-day contracts, which makes hub congestion a more common source of delay. The desk should check the `eta` confidence and mention a low confidence plainly.

## Temperature-controlled option

Bellmoor offers a temperature-controlled option on CC-22 for loads that must stay between 2 and 8 degrees. The option must be booked in advance and costs an additional charge shown on the shipper's rate card. It cannot be added to a consignment that is already in transit. If a shipper asks to add it mid-journey, explain that it is not possible and suggest booking it on the next shipment. A reroute of a temperature-controlled load is only accepted into a hub that has a chilled bay, which the hub directory notes.

## Liability

Liability under CC-22 is limited to 12.00 per kilogram of the damaged, lost or short-delivered goods, based on the gross weight on the delivery note. This is higher than the other Tallowgate contracts because Bellmoor carries a heavier insurance programme. The limit does not cover consequential loss. A shipper who needs more cover for high-value freight should discuss declared-value insurance with their account manager before booking.

## Inspection and claim window

The consignee must inspect the goods on delivery and report visible damage within 48 hours. Damage reported after 48 hours may still be claimed, but the claims team will ask for stronger evidence that the damage happened in transit. A claim must be lodged within 21 days of delivery, or from the expected delivery date for lost goods. The cargo claims procedure explains the evidence required and how the claim is reviewed.

## Alternate hub arrangements

Each CC-22 consignment records an alternate hub. For TG-604227 the alternate is Tarnwick Gate (TRB) and for TG-604590 it is Orsley Park (ORS), although TG-604590 has already been delivered and cannot be rerouted. A reroute is only possible when the alternate hub is open and accepts reroutes, and it always needs a human approval. Holmquay (HLM), the usual home hub for CC-22 port traffic, is often congested at peak, which is the most common reason a CC-22 shipment is delayed there.

## What the desk should say

Quote the two-day service, the 12.00 per kilogram liability, the 48-hour damage inspection and the 21-day claim window. Do not promise delivery times beyond the `eta` record.
