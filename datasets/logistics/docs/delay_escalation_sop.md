# Delay Escalation SOP

## Purpose

Delays are normal in freight, but silent delays are not. This procedure sets out when a late shipment is escalated, who is told, and how fast the shipper hears about it. It applies to all shipments with the status delayed or held.

## Escalation thresholds

Time is counted from the end of the original ETA window, not from the booking date.

- **More than 24 hours late**: the shipment is escalated to the shipper's account manager. The account manager owns the conversation with the shipper from this point.
- **More than 48 hours late**: the shipment is escalated again, to the hub operations lead for the hub where the shipment currently sits. The lead decides whether a reroute, a dedicated vehicle or a partial release is appropriate.
- **More than 72 hours late**: the operations director is informed and a written recovery plan is required by the end of the next working day.

The operations desk is staffed around the clock, so the clock keeps running at night and at weekends. Account managers work Monday to Friday, 7:00 to 19:00. A delay that crosses the 24 hour mark outside those hours is queued for the account manager and the on-duty desk supervisor makes the first call.

## Notifying the shipper

Once a shipment is escalated, the shipper must be told within 2 hours. The message states the delay reason, the new ETA window, the confidence level and the next review time. If the shipper has a stated contact preference on file (for example portal updates only, or a call for delays above a set length), follow it.

## ETA confidence

Every ETA carries one of three confidence levels:

- **high**: the shipment is on a normal lane with no known blockers. The window is unlikely to move.
- **medium**: there is a known disruption, but a recovery plan is in place. The window may move by up to half a day.
- **low**: the cause is unresolved, such as a berth queue, a closed yard or a missing linehaul. The window is a best estimate and may move by a day or more.

When confidence is low, say so to the shipper and promise an update at the next review rather than a firm time.

## Delay reasons

The delay reason is recorded as a short code, for example berth_queue_at_HLM, yard_closure_at_WYN or linehaul_driver_shortage. Quote the reason in plain words when speaking to a shipper. If the reason is a Tallowgate hub closure, mention that reroute fees are waived under the Reroute SOP.

## Review rhythm

Escalated shipments are reviewed at 08:00 and 16:00 every day until delivered. Each review adds a note to the shipment record, even if nothing has changed, so the account manager can see the shipment has not been forgotten.
