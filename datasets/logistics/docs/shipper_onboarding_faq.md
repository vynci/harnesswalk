# Shipper Onboarding FAQ

This page answers the questions new shippers ask most often when they start using Tallowgate Freight. If your question is not here, the operations desk can help at any hour, and your account manager can help during office hours (Monday to Friday, 7:00 to 19:00).

## How do I open an account?

Account opening starts on the customer portal. You provide your company details, the lanes you expect to ship on, typical monthly volumes and the type of cargo. A Tallowgate account manager then reviews the application, usually within two working days, and confirms the carrier contract that best fits your lanes. Once the contract is signed, your portal login is activated and you can book your first consignment.

## How are contract codes assigned?

Every account is linked to a carrier contract, shown as a code such as CC-17, CC-22 or CC-31. The code identifies the carrier arrangement behind your lane: the service level, the liability per kilogram and the claim terms. Your account manager chooses the code during onboarding, based on your cargo and the regions you ship between. The summaries for each contract are in the knowledge base. For example, CC-17 carries liability of 8.50 per kilogram, CC-22 carries 12.00 and CC-31 carries 6.00.

## How is the alternate hub chosen?

Each contract record names an alternate hub. This is the hub a shipment may be moved to when its current hub is held, congested or closed. The alternate hub is picked for its position on your lane and its spare capacity, and it is fixed on the contract. The assistant will only reroute a shipment through that listed hub, and only when the hub is not closed and accepts reroutes. Every reroute also needs a human approval before it takes effect.

## Where do I find my shipment ids?

Shipment ids look like TG-604118: the letters TG, a dash and six digits. You will find yours on the booking confirmation, in the customer portal under your open consignments and on every status update. If you cannot find one, ask the operations desk and give the origin, destination and booking date, and they will help you locate it.

## What are the invoice cycles?

Invoices are issued per consignment on delivery and are payable within 30 days of the invoice date. Shippers with high volumes can ask for a consolidated monthly invoice. Surcharges, such as the peak season surcharge, appear as separate lines so you can see exactly what you are paying for.

## How does the customer portal work?

The portal shows your shipments, their status, the current hub and the ETA window. You can book collections, download proof of delivery, log a cargo claim and set how you prefer to receive updates. Status changes appear in the portal as soon as a hub scans the freight. If you prefer, you can ask the operations desk or the assistant for the same information at any time.

## Who do I call about a delay?

The operations desk runs 24/7 and can explain a delay, check the ETA and tell you whether a reroute is possible. If a shipment is more than 24 hours late, it is escalated to your account manager, who will contact you. Always have the shipment id ready.
