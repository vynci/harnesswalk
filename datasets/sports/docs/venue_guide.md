# Venue Guide

This guide describes the four grounds used by the Copperline Community League during the 2026 autumn season. Check the fixture list for the venue of each match, and read the postponement policy for what happens when a pitch is unfit. All kick-off times are local.

## Harbour Park

Harbour Park is home to Ashquill Rovers and Millcress Athletic and also hosts the Copperline Cup final on 2026-12-19. The pitch is a 3G artificial surface, so it drains quickly and is rarely affected by rain. Players must wear moulded studs or flat trainers; metal studs are not allowed on the 3G surface. Parking is in a small car park behind the east stand, with room for about 40 cars. It fills up early on Saturdays, so visiting supporters are asked to use the street spaces on the harbour side as well and to leave the car park entrance clear for the ambulance.

## Quarry Lane

Quarry Lane is the shared home ground of Norrowby United and Quarrimoor Town. It has a natural grass pitch that drains slowly after heavy rain, which is why many weather postponements in the league involve this ground. The groundskeeper inspects the pitch at first light on match day and reports to the referee. There is a roadside lay-by with space for about 25 cars and a further overflow field that is opened only on cup days. Spectators stand along the touchline behind the rope; the far side is closed to the public.

## Old Mill Ground

Old Mill Ground is the home of Rivelmere Wanderers, and Tanswold Park also plays its home fixtures here when the Tannery pitch is being reseeded. The pitch is natural grass, and the goalmouths are replaced with fresh turf every winter break. There is no car park: parking is on the surrounding streets only, and neighbours ask visitors not to block driveways. The nearest bus stop is a five minute walk from the main gate.

## Ridge Meadow

Ridge Meadow is the home ground of Brackenwhist FC and Lowfennel Albion. The pitch is a hybrid grass surface, natural turf reinforced with artificial fibres, which holds up well in wet weather and rarely needs a postponement. The large car park holds about 120 cars and has marked disabled bays near the main gate. The ground has a covered stand on the north side with seating for about 150 people.

## Changing rooms

Every venue has two team changing rooms and one separate changing room for match officials. Home teams use the room nearest the main gate; away teams use the room on the far side of the clubhouse. Changing rooms are unlocked 60 minutes before kick-off and must be left tidy and locked within 45 minutes of the final whistle. Valuables should be kept with the team captain, as the league cannot accept responsibility for lost items.

## Accessibility

Harbour Park and Ridge Meadow have step-free access from the car park to the touchline and an accessible toilet in the clubhouse. Quarry Lane has a firm path from the lay-by to the halfway line, but the ground is uneven in wet weather. Old Mill Ground has a gravel path and a single step at the clubhouse door; a portable ramp is available from the home club on request. Supporters who need help should contact the home club's captain before match day.
