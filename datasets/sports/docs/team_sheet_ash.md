# Team Sheet: Ashquill Rovers

This team sheet lists the registered squad of Ashquill Rovers (club code ASH) for the 2026 autumn season. Ashquill Rovers play in the premier division and use Harbour Park as their home ground. Ashquill Rovers lead the premier division on 22 points after eight matches. The sheet is kept by the club captain and checked by the league office at the start of each season.

## Club details

Ashquill Rovers play in claret shirts with white shorts, and their away kit is plain white. Home matches are usually scheduled on Saturday afternoons at Harbour Park. The squad is registered at 14 players, which leaves two free places under the squad limit of 16 players. The club captain is the first contact for all questions to the league office.

## Changes to the squad

The club captain must tell the league office about any signing or departure before it can take effect. A new player cannot appear on a matchday sheet until the registration is confirmed. The league office answers captains Monday to Thursday, 18:00 to 21:00.
