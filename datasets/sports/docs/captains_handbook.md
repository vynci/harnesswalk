# Captains Handbook

Every Copperline Community League club names one captain who is the club's contact with the league office, the referee and the opposing captain. This handbook sets out what that role involves during the 2026 autumn season.

## Before the match

Submit your team sheet to the referee 60 minutes before kick-off. List the starting eleven and the named substitutes by player id. Only registered players may appear, and the matchday squad is at most 16 players. Check that every player on your sheet is eligible and not serving a ban; a player who plays while banned can cost the club points.

Arrive in time to inspect the pitch with the referee if a weather call is needed. The league makes its postponement call by 10:00 on match day, and the office tells both captains.

## During the match

Matches are two halves of 40 minutes with rolling substitutions, so you may bring players on and off more than once. Speak to the referee calmly, and keep your players within the Code of Conduct. As captain you are the only player who should approach the referee to ask about a decision. Cards are recorded by the referee and counted by the league office.

## Cards and bans

Track your players' yellow cards yourself. Five yellow cards in a season earn a one-match ban. A red card carries a ban of at least two matches. The league office confirms every ban to the captain, and you are responsible for telling the player. If you believe a card record is wrong, raise it with the office promptly.

## Registrations

Registering a new player is done through the league office, not through the volunteer help desk. The help desk cannot register, transfer or suspend anyone. Read the Registration and Eligibility Rules for the deadlines and for what proof of eligibility is needed. Keep your squad list up to date, because the team sheets published by the league are built from it.

## Contacting the league office

The league office answers captains Monday to Thursday, 18:00 to 21:00. Outside those hours, leave your question with the help desk, which can answer rules questions from the league documents and look up fixtures, the table and player statistics. Anything that needs a decision, such as an appeal, a registration, a rearranged fixture or a disciplinary matter, goes to the office.

## After the match

Check that the referee's report records the correct score and scorers. The report is due at the league office within 24 hours, and the league table is updated once it arrives. If you spot an error, tell the office early so that it can be corrected before the next table update.

## Representing the club

Captains set the tone for their club. Welcome the opposing captain and the officials, make sure your supporters know the rules for the venue, and report any incident involving spectators to the office the same day. The code of conduct applies to players, captains, coaches and supporters alike.
