# Fixtures and Postponement Policy

This policy explains how fixtures are set for the Copperline Community League, when a match may be postponed and what happens when a team cannot field enough players. It applies to league matches in both divisions. Cup matches follow the cup competition rules.

## How fixtures are set

The league office publishes the full fixture list before the start of the season. Each club plays every other club in its division twice, once at home and once away. Fixtures are played on Saturdays and Sundays, with kick-offs between 11:00 and 16:00, local time. A club's home matches are played at the venue listed for that club in the venue guide. The fixture list gives the date, kick-off time, opponent and venue. A published fixture can only be moved by the league office.

## Weather and pitch conditions

Postponement decisions for weather and pitch conditions are made by 10:00 on match day. The home club's captain inspects the pitch and phones the referee and the league office with a recommendation. The referee makes the final decision on the day. If the venue is a natural grass pitch and has standing water or is frozen, the match is likely to be postponed. The 3G artificial pitch at Harbour Park is rarely affected by rain but may be postponed for ice, snow or flooding. The league office publishes any postponement on the fixtures list and informs both captains.

## What if no decision is made by 10:00

If no postponement has been announced by 10:00, the match is on. Teams must travel and be ready to play. If the referee finds the pitch unplayable on arrival, they may still postpone the match, but the away club's reasonable travel costs may be claimed from the league's contingency fund.

## Rearranging a postponed match

A postponed match must be rearranged within 14 days of the original date. Both captains agree a new date and kick-off time and send it to the league office for approval. If the captains cannot agree within seven days, the league office sets the date. A match postponed twice is played at the venue of the club that had its home fixture the first time, and may be moved to a neutral venue if necessary.

## Postponement by agreement

Two captains may agree to postpone a match for reasons other than weather, such as a clash with a family event or a lack of players. Both must confirm the agreement in writing to the league office at least 48 hours before kick-off, and the new date must be within 14 days of the original.

## Minimum players and forfeits

A match may only start if each team has at least 8 players present, unless both captains agree otherwise. If a team has fewer than 7 players ready 15 minutes after the published kick-off, it forfeits the match. The forfeit is recorded as a 3-0 win for the opposing team, and the team forfeiting is fined. If a team has fewer than 7 players on the pitch at any point during the match, the referee abandons it and the same result applies.

## Late arrivals

If a team arrives late but before the 15-minute limit, the match is played and the referee reduces the first half if there is not enough daylight. The referee reports the late arrival to the league office. Repeated lateness may lead to a fine or a points deduction.

## Contacting the league office

Captains may contact the league office, Monday to Thursday, 18:00 to 21:00, about fixtures, postponements and rearranged dates. The volunteer help desk can report published fixture details but cannot postpone or rearrange a match.
