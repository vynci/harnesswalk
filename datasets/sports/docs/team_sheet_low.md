# Team Sheet: Lowfennel Albion

This sheet lists the registered squad of Lowfennel Albion (club code LOW) for the 2026 autumn season. Lowfennel Albion play in the championship division. The sheet is kept by the league office and is updated whenever a registration changes.

## Club details

Lowfennel Albion share their home ground, Ridge Meadow, with Brackenwhist FC (BRK). Ridge Meadow has a hybrid grass pitch and a large car park. Because two clubs use the ground, the league office schedules their home fixtures on different days or at different kick-off times where it can. This autumn both clubs play at Ridge Meadow on 2026-10-11, with Lowfennel Albion travelling to Brackenwhist FC for a 13:30 kick-off.

Club colours are mustard and charcoal. The captain this season is Nell Tarrant.

## Disciplinary position

Nell Tarrant has reached five yellow cards this season. Under the Discipline Code, five yellow cards in a season earn a one-match ban, so she will miss Lowfennel Albion's next fixture and return afterwards. The league office confirms bans to the club captain; the help desk only reports the card count.

Other players on the squad currently have fewer than three yellow cards. Captains are expected to track their own players' totals and to tell the league office early if they think a record is wrong.

## Team sheet rules for this club

Lowfennel Albion must submit their team sheet to the match referee 60 minutes before kick-off, listing the starting eleven and the named substitutes by player id. Substitutions are rolling, so players may go on and off more than once during a match. A player who is not on the registered list above may not take part, even as a substitute.

## Contact

Questions about this squad go to the league office through the club captain. The office answers captains Monday to Thursday, 18:00 to 21:00. The help desk can look up fixtures, the league table and individual player statistics, but it cannot register, transfer or suspend players.
