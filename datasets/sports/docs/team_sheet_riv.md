# Team Sheet: Rivelmere Wanderers

This team sheet lists the registered squad of Rivelmere Wanderers (club code RIV) for the 2026 autumn season. Rivelmere Wanderers play in the championship division and use Old Mill Ground as their home ground. They lead the championship with 20 points after eight matches.

## Club details

Rivelmere Wanderers play in green and white hooped shirts, and their away kit is black. Old Mill Ground is a natural grass pitch with street parking only, so the club asks visiting teams to arrive early and share cars where possible. Tanswold Park also plays home fixtures at Old Mill Ground when its own pitch is being reseeded, so the club coordinates the match calendar with them. The squad is registered at 15 players, leaving one free place under the squad limit of 16.

## Notes on key players

Lena Harrowgate (PL-203) has eight goals and four assists in eight appearances and was named championship player of the month for September. She has two yellow cards this season. Bram Okonjo (PL-201) is the first-choice goalkeeper and the club captain. Ottilie Strand (PL-209) is the club's main passer from midfield.

## Changes to the squad

The captain must report any signing or departure to the league office before the change takes effect. A player cannot appear on a matchday sheet until the registration is confirmed. The league office answers captains Monday to Thursday, 18:00 to 21:00.
