# Registration and Eligibility Rules

These rules say who may play for a club in the Copperline Community League, how players are registered and when the registration window closes. They apply to both divisions for the 2026 autumn season. The league office handles registration questions from captains Monday to Thursday, 18:00 to 21:00. The volunteer help desk cannot register or transfer a player.

## Who may play

A player must be at least 16 years old on the day of their first league appearance. There is no upper age limit. A player must be registered with the league and with exactly one club for the season. A player who appears for a club without being registered is ineligible, and the club may lose the match.

## One club per season

A player may be registered with only one club in a season. Moving between the premier and championship clubs is treated as a transfer, and transfers are not permitted once the registration window has closed, except in the exceptional cases described below. A player who plays for two clubs in the same season is banned for the remainder of the season and both clubs may be fined.

## Registration window

The registration window closes on 2026-09-20. Every player who will take part in the autumn season must be registered by that date. Registrations are submitted by the club captain or secretary on the league registration form. A player registered after the window closes can only be added with the written approval of the league office, and only if the club's squad has fewer than 16 registered players at that point.

## Player ids

On successful registration the league issues a player id. Ids use the format PL-123: the letters PL, a hyphen and three digits. The id stays with the player for the whole season and appears on team sheets, referee reports and the player statistics record. Captains should use the player id, not the name, in all correspondence, because two players may share a name.

## ID check on first appearance

Every player must show photo identification to the referee before their first league appearance. Acceptable identification is a passport, a driving licence, a school or college card with a photograph, or the league's own player card. The referee notes that the check has been made on the match report. A player who cannot show identification may play only if the opposing captain agrees and the referee records it, and the player must present identification to the league office within seven days.

## Exceptions and late signings

The league office may allow a late registration or transfer in these cases: a long-term injury that leaves a club without a goalkeeper, a club falling below 11 registered players, or a player returning to the area after a period away. Each exception needs a written request from the captain stating the player's id or name, the reason and the date. Exceptions are approved by the league committee and are never retroactive.

## Squad lists and team sheets

A club may register more than 16 players but may name only 16 on its team sheet for any one match. Team sheets must be submitted before kick-off. Each team sheet lists the player ids and positions for the starters and the substitutes.

## Keeping records up to date

Captains must tell the league office about any change in a player's contact details, club or availability. Players who leave a club mid-season may not be registered with another club until the following season.
