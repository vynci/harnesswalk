# Team Sheet: Norrowby United

This team sheet lists the registered squad of Norrowby United (club code NOR) for the 2026 autumn season. Norrowby United play in the premier division and share their home ground, Quarry Lane, with Quarrimoor Town. The club is second in the premier division with 19 points after eight matches.

## Club details

Norrowby United play in navy shirts with sky blue sleeves, and their away kit is yellow. Because Quarry Lane is shared, the club's home fixtures are scheduled around Quarrimoor Town's, and the two clubs agree a rota for the changing rooms. Quarry Lane drains slowly after heavy rain, so Norrowby United check the pitch condition early on match day and wait for the weather call, which is made by 10:00. The squad is registered at 13 players, leaving three free places under the squad limit of 16.

## Notes on key players

Pavel Orsini (PL-137) is the club's leading scorer with six goals in seven appearances and has not been booked this season. Annika Rostrevor (PL-131) is the first-choice goalkeeper and also the club captain, so she handles all communication with the league office. Callum Eddowes (PL-139) takes the corners and free kicks.

## Changes to the squad

Any signing or departure must be reported by the captain to the league office before the player can appear on a matchday sheet. The league office answers captains Monday to Thursday, 18:00 to 21:00, and confirms each change in writing by the next working day.
