# Season Calendar 2026 Autumn

This calendar lists the key dates of the Copperline Community League autumn season. The league office publishes individual fixture dates separately; this page gives the shape of the season. Matches are two halves of 40 minutes, and all kick-off times are local.

## Season start

The season starts on 2026-09-05. The first round of fixtures is played that weekend in both divisions, the premier division and the championship division. Each division has four clubs, and every club plays each of the others more than once across the autumn.

## Regular fixtures

Most matches take place on Saturdays and Sundays, with kick-off times between 11:00 and 16:00. Examples this autumn include Ashquill Rovers against Millcress Athletic at Harbour Park on 2026-10-10 at 14:30, Norrowby United against Quarrimoor Town at Quarry Lane on 2026-10-10 at 16:00, Rivelmere Wanderers against Tanswold Park at Old Mill Ground on 2026-10-11 at 11:00, and Brackenwhist FC against Lowfennel Albion at Ridge Meadow on 2026-10-11 at 13:30. For any specific club, the fixtures lookup gives the next match with its time and venue.

## Mid-season break

The mid-season break runs from 2026-11-14 to 2026-11-22. No league matches are played in that period. Clubs may use the break for friendlies and training, but friendlies do not count towards league records or cards. Squad registrations may still be processed by the league office during the break, subject to the Registration and Eligibility Rules.

## Final league day

The final league day is 2026-12-12. All matches in both divisions are played on that day so that no club has an advantage from knowing other results. Kick-off times are set by the league office and published after the penultimate round. The final league table is confirmed once the last match reports have been received.

## Cup final

The Copperline Cup final is on 2026-12-19 at Harbour Park. The two finalists are decided under the Cup Rules. Harbour Park has an artificial pitch, so the final is unlikely to be affected by weather, but the 10:00 weather call still applies on match day.

## Weather and rearranged matches

If a match is postponed, the league office rearranges it and tells both captains. Rearranged matches are normally played on the next free date before the final league day. Postponement calls are made by 10:00 on match day.

## Contacting the league office

Captains can ask about dates through the league office, Monday to Thursday, 18:00 to 21:00. The volunteer help desk can look up fixtures, the table and player statistics, and can answer rules questions from the league documents.
