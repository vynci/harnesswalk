# Product sheet: Trailhead 28 backpack

The Trailhead 28 is a fictional daypack sold by Sample Shop. The figures below are synthetic.

## Specifications

- Volume: 28 litres
- Weight: 0.9 kg
- Fabric: recycled ripstop nylon with a water-resistant coating
- Laptop sleeve: fits a 16 inch laptop
- Colours: slate, moss and ember
- Price: 89 dollars

## Care

Wipe with a damp cloth. Do not machine wash or tumble dry. Let it air dry out of direct sun.

## Warranty

The Trailhead 28 has a 2 year warranty against defects in materials and workmanship. Wear and
tear, and damage from misuse, are not covered. Warranty claims go through support with the
order id and a photo of the defect.
