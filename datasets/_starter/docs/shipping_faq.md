# Shipping FAQ

Answers to common delivery questions at Sample Shop, a fictional shop. All content is
synthetic.

## How long does processing take?

Orders are packed within 1 to 2 business days. An order shows as processing until the parcel
leaves the warehouse in Toronto, then as shipped with a tracking number.

## How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days after shipping inside Canada and 5 to 8 business
days to the United States. Express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days and costs 15 dollars.

## Can I change the delivery address?

The address can be changed while an order is processing. Once an order has shipped, the
carrier can hold it at a pickup point but cannot redirect it.

## What if my parcel is late?

If a parcel is more than 3 business days past its expected arrival date, contact support with
the order id. Support opens a carrier trace and, after 10 business days, sends a replacement or
a refund.
