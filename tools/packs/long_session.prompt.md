# Pack brief: long_session (Fernhollow Journeys)

## The business

Fernhollow Journeys is a small fictional travel agency that plans trips to four invented
destinations: the Isle of Carrow (an island reached by the Carrow Ferry Company from Mirelake
harbour, and in winter from Port Ellery on Friday to Monday), Mirelake (a lakeside town with
the ferry harbour and the regional rail station), the Brannock Highlands (hills, walking, a
lodge) and Port Ellery (a coastal town with a guesthouse and beaches). Partner hotels: HT-12
Carrow Harbour Inn, HT-15 Mirelake Quay Hotel, HT-21 Brannock Lodge, HT-30 Ellery Sands
Guesthouse. The desk is staffed 08:00 to 18:00, Monday to Friday. The core files are given to
you; the documents must agree with them exactly.

## The feature

Long sessions: travellers plan over long chats, so old turns are compacted into a running
summary; and durable memory: preferences are remembered across sessions. The documents are the
reference material the assistant cites.

## Facts the documents must state (use these numbers exactly)

Ferries (winter timetable 1 November 2026 to 31 March 2027): MIR-CAR departs 08:15, 12:30,
16:45 and 23:30 (the overnight sailing, arriving 01:10, cabins 30.00 extra), crossing 1 h 40
min; CAR-MIR departs 07:00, 11:15, 16:45, 21:00; ELL-CAR departs 09:30 and 15:00, crossing
2 h 55 min, Friday to Monday only in winter. No ferries on 25 December 2026. Fares: foot
passenger 18.00 (26.00 from Port Ellery), car 64.00 (88.00 from Port Ellery). Children under 5
travel free, children 5 to 15 pay half the foot fare. Tickets can be changed free up to 2 hours
before departure; after that a change costs 5.00.

Luggage on the ferry: two bags of up to 23 kg per foot passenger free; bicycles 6.00 each and
must be booked; dogs travel free on deck or in a car, not in cabins; no luggage limit in a car.

Booking terms (Fernhollow Journeys): a trip is confirmed when the 20 percent deposit is paid;
the balance is due 14 days before departure; cancelling more than 30 days before departure
refunds everything but the deposit; 30 to 14 days before, 50 percent is refunded; under 14
days, nothing is refunded; hotel cancellation terms of each partner hotel apply on top; the
desk makes changes within one working day.

Seasonal calendar: the winter timetable dates; Carrow's harbour festival on 12 December 2026
(extra 19:00 sailing that day); the Brannock Highlands trails close above the snow line from
1 December; Port Ellery's beach huts close from 1 November to 31 March; the Mirelake winter
market on Saturdays in December.

Accessibility: HT-15 has 4 accessible rooms, HT-30 has 2, HT-12 has 1, HT-21 none; the ferry
has a lift to the passenger deck and two wheelchair spaces per sailing (book 48 hours ahead);
the Carrow harbour bus is low floor.

Travel documents: photo ID for every adult on the ferry; children's birth certificate copies
are not needed; a travel insurance policy is the traveller's own choice and the desk only
notes whether one exists.

## Documents

Write the documents the docs plan lists. Do not write any phone number, email address, web
address or street address. Do not name real places, companies or brands.
