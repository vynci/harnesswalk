# Pack brief: helpdesk_lite (Pebblebrook IT Helpdesk)

## The business

Pebblebrook Studio is a small fictional design studio with three offices: north, harbour and
east. Its IT desk is open 08:00 to 18:00, Monday to Friday (at the harbour office 09:00 to 17:00,
Monday to Thursday; the east office has a visiting technician on Wednesdays). Urgent outages
outside desk hours go to the on-call technician through the support form. The core files are
given to you; the documents must agree with them exactly.

## The feature

Short lookups on the lowest rung of the harness: the pack runs with no guardrails, no
knowledge retrieval and no memory, so the documents matter only when a learner moves the pack
up the ladder. Keep them short, practical and consistent with the tool tables.

## Facts the documents must state (use these numbers exactly)

- Passwords: reset on the self-service account portal in about 5 minutes; at least 14
  characters; changed every 12 months; a locked account unlocks after 15 minutes; the desk
  never asks for a password by chat, email or phone.
- Tickets: opened through the support form; the desk answers within 4 working hours; statuses
  open, in_progress, waiting_on_you, resolved; a ticket waiting on you closes after 5 working
  days without a reply.
- VPN: needed for the file share from home; the app reconnects by itself; the gateway
  replacement on 2026-11-07 (outage from 08:20, until 12:00); workaround: the office network or
  the web apps.
- Printers: NO-PRN-1 and NO-PRN-2 (north), HB-PRN-1 and HB-PRN-2 (harbour), EA-PRN-1 (east);
  a stuck job clears when cancelled and sent again; toner is ordered by the desk.
- Wifi: staff network Pebble-Staff; guest networks Pebble-Guest-North, Pebble-Guest-Harbour,
  Pebble-Guest-East; the guest code is on the reception card and changes every Monday.
- File share: backed up nightly 01:00 to 02:00; deleted files can be restored for 30 days.
- Video calls: kits in Granite, Slate, Tide, Jetty and Orchard; echo is fixed by using a headset
  or muting the room speaker.
- Equipment loans: spare laptops and headsets from the north office for up to two weeks.
- New starters: the manager opens a ticket; the laptop is ready two working days later; accounts
  are created the day before the start date.
- Security basics: lock the screen when leaving the desk; report a suspicious message through
  the support form; never plug in an unknown USB stick.

## Documents

Write the documents the docs plan lists. Do not write any phone number, email address, web
address or street address. Do not name real places, companies, brands or products.
